Five ways to be the government.

The same nation, the same engine, five personalities. Every variant preserves the core — you play the government, not the country: declare intent → the world pushes back → a bounded change → an in-world headline. All use Facet glass, all are one navigator, all show bands, never raw numbers. Pick the feel; the plumbing is shared.

What every variant demonstrates (from bible v2)
  • Executive, not nation. You act as the government; the country is what you steer.
  • Intent-first, instant by default. Declare a goal in plain words; commit in one click; cooldown (not scheduling) paces you. Deliberation is opt-in.
  • The canonical loop, always closed. Every action → a bounded stat change → an in-world headline (toast) → a ledger row. No open loops.
  • Bands, never numbers. Standing and relations read “Cooperative / Restless / Unclear,” and P2P relations are player-overridable.
  • Fog of information. Under-staffed ministries mask estimates (Treasury reads “Unclear”; provinces sit under fog) until you spend capacity.
  • Governance is legible. The record shows what changed and why — Burg’s guarantee, for free.
