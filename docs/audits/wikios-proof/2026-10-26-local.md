## Note on the bench (added after the run)

`bench.failures.wikios` (50 of 200) is every WikiOS article page request of the bench, 25 pages × 2 runs, failing with
"The response redirected too many times"; that is also why `page+data.totalMs` has no samples. The dev server's Clerk
development instance redirects a request with no session cookie and `accept: text/html` to its handshake, and the
bench keeps no cookies. Production keys do not: a cookie-less `text/html` request for the live
`maps.ixwiki.com/dashboard` answers 200. The local profile does not gate the bench; the launch profile does (runbook
step 7e). The numbers above are as the run measured them.
