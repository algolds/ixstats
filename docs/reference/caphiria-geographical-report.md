# Geographical report for Caphiria

## Executive summary

Caphiria covers 4,723,109 square kilometers, which equals 1,823,602 square miles. This represents roughly 3.17 percent of Earth's total land area, making it comparable in total surface area to India. The territory spans 3,591 kilometers from north to south and 3,929 kilometers from east to west in the southern hemisphere.

The national perimeter measures 23,978 kilometers. Marine coastline accounts for 23,843 kilometers, or 99.4 percent of this boundary. The land consists of 61 discrete polygons, comprising one large continental mainland of 4,273,127 square kilometers and 60 offshore islands totaling 449,982 square kilometers. Terrestrial frontiers total 135 kilometers, bordering Cartadania for 130 kilometers to the northwest and Pelaxia for 5 kilometers to the southwest.

Beyond the mainland core, Caphiria exercises sovereignty over six external possessions and dependencies totaling 1,137,380 square kilometers: Escal Isles, Maristella, Zaclaria, Vespera, Amaniaris, and Shenendehowa Bay.

The mainland is predominantly low-lying. Coastal lowlands under 100 meters cover 52.8 percent of the country, while low hills between 100 and 349 meters cover 28.7 percent. The mean national elevation is 211 meters above sea level, with elevations ranging from sea level to a maximum of 3,999 meters.

The climate is largely dry. Steppe and semiarid conditions cover 33.6 percent of the country, and arid desert conditions cover another 33.6 percent. Subtropical dry summer conditions cover 16.1 percent along the coasts, while tropical wet-and-dry zones account for 9.5 percent. Smaller pockets of tropical wet, highland, subtropical humid, and boreal climates cover the remainder. The mean annual temperature is 19.6°C, and annual precipitation averages 322 millimeters.

Hydrology includes 58 primary river channels with a collective length of 44,441 kilometers within the borders. Twenty-six major lakes cover 38,476.5 square kilometers, representing 0.81 percent of the national area. Drainage density is 0.009 kilometers per square kilometer.

### Drainage patterns

The low drainage density of 0.009 kilometers per square kilometer reflects the large share of semiarid and desert terrain in the interior. Most rivers drain westward into the Odoneru Ocean, northeastward into the Sea of Canete, or southward toward the Kindreds Sea and Pelaxian Gulf.

The 26 mapped lakes cover a combined 38,476.5 square kilometers. These water bodies occupy low-lying coastal depressions, tectonic fault basins along the eastern margins, and interior drainage sinks.
