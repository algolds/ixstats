This Privacy Policy explains how **Alpaia Holdings**, a New York general partnership (“**Alpaia Holdings**”, “**IxStates**”, “**we**”, “**us**”, or “**our**”), handles personal information when you use IxStates, IxWiki, and the other services described in our [Terms of Service](/terms) (the “**Services**”), including through `ixwiki.com` and its subdomains.

Alpaia Holdings is the controller of your personal information. You can reach us about anything in this policy at [admin@ixwiki.com](mailto:admin@ixwiki.com).

## 1. Information We Collect {#data-collected}

> **Plain English Summary:** Your account details, any accounts you choose to link, what you create in the Services, your messages, payment records if you buy something, and the technical logs that keep the server safe.

### 1.1 Account Information

Sign-in is handled by Clerk. We receive and store your Clerk user ID, email address, username, and when your account was created and updated. If you sign in with a third-party account such as Discord, Clerk passes us the identifiers that account shares.

### 1.2 Linked Accounts (IxnayID)

If you choose to link other accounts, we store:

- **Discord:** your Discord user ID and username, for role sync and bot notifications.
- **IxWiki (MediaWiki):** your wiki user ID and username, for edit attribution.
- **Old forum (XenForo):** your old forum member ID and name, to credit your imported forum posts.
- **NationStates:** your verified nation name, for card imports and ownership checks.

### 1.3 What You Create

We store the content and simulation state you produce: nations and their statistics and policies, cabinets and personas, ThinkPages posts and comments, IxWiki edits, maps, Onoma lexicons, cards and trades, and achievements. Most of this is public by design (see Section 3.1).

### 1.4 Private Messages

We store ThinkShare direct messages so we can deliver them. They are encrypted in transit but are **not end-to-end encrypted**. Administrators do not read them as a matter of course. They access a conversation only when a participant reports it, when investigating a breach of the Terms, to protect the security of the Services, or when the law requires it.

### 1.5 Payment Information

If you buy something under Section 5.5 of the Terms, the payment provider named at checkout collects your payment details. We receive a record of the purchase (what you bought, the amount, the date, and the status) and a limited card summary, such as the brand and last four digits. We never receive or store your full card number.

### 1.6 Technical and Security Data

When your browser talks to our servers, we record your IP address, browser user agent, and the pages requested, in server access logs, rate-limit caches, and security audit records. IxWiki also records the IP address of each edit for abuse investigation, visible only to authorised administrators.

### 1.7 Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views in aggregate. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites.

### 1.8 Information from Other Sources

When a realm imports nation pages from other wikis, such as IIWiki or the Alternative History wiki, we may receive the public username of the page's author along with the content. Section 2.5 of the Terms covers imported content.

Some features send content to third-party AI services to generate output, for example fictional match events for sports commentary. We send only what the feature needs, never your email or account details, and we use only providers whose terms bar them from training on data sent through their services. We do not use your content to train AI models.

### 2.2 No Sale, No Advertising

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are used in California law. We do not use personal information to build advertising profiles.

We may disclose information when the law requires it, or when necessary to protect the safety of users or the public or to defend our legal rights. If the Services are transferred to a new operator, your information may go with them, and that operator must honour this policy.

## 4. Cookies & Browser Storage {#cookies-local-storage}

We use only cookies and storage that the Services need to work. There are no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

- **Clerk session cookies** keep you signed in.
- **Cloudflare security cookies** help tell real visitors from bots.
- **Browser local storage** remembers preferences on your device, such as your selected country, theme, and panel layout. It never leaves your browser.

## 5. Your Rights & Account Deletion {#user-rights-erasure}

> **Plain English Summary:** You can see, correct, export, or delete your personal information. If you delete your account, your login and personal details are erased, and your public posts stay credited to "a former member" so that conversations still make sense.

### 5.1 Your Rights

Wherever you live, you may ask us to:

- **Access** the personal information we hold about you and get a copy;
- **Correct** inaccurate information (most of it you can edit in your account settings);
- **Export** your content, such as nation summaries and Onoma lexicons, in a portable format;
- **Delete** your account and personal information;
- **Object to or restrict** processing based on legitimate interests; and
- **Withdraw consent** for anything that relies on it.

You can download your data and delete your account yourself under **Settings → Privacy & Security**. For anything else, email [admin@ixwiki.com](mailto:admin@ixwiki.com) from the address on your account. We may ask you to confirm your identity, and we respond within one month (or forty-five (45) days where California law applies). We will not treat you differently for exercising these rights. If you are in the EU or UK and are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

### 5.2 What Happens When You Delete Your Account

When you ask to delete your account, we wait fourteen (14) days before erasing it, in case the request was a mistake or someone else used your account. You can cancel at any time during those days in your settings. After that:

1. **Erased:** your Clerk account, email address, linked-account associations, payment provider customer link, and private messages you sent.
2. **Anonymised:** your public posts, personas, and contributions to Collaborative Works stay, unlinked from your account and credited to "a former member" (Terms, Sections 2.9 and 6.1).
3. **Removed:** your Nation Material, under Section 2.7 of the Terms. The Shared Record stays.
4. **IxWiki:** your edits stay under CC BY-SA. On request we rename your wiki account to an anonymous name.
5. **Kept where required:** purchase records needed for tax and accounting, and records tied to an open moderation case or legal claim, for as long as they are required.

Erasure runs automatically when the fourteen days are up. Copies in backups expire on their normal schedule.

## 6. Security, Transfers & Contact {#security-contact}

### 6.1 Security

Connections to the Services are encrypted with TLS. Production servers are reachable only by authorised administrators using SSH keys, and databases are not exposed to the public internet. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you, and the authorities, as the law requires.

### 6.2 International Transfers

We are based in the United States, and our servers and most of our providers are there. If you use the Services from elsewhere, your information is transferred to the United States. Where the law of your country requires safeguards for that transfer, we rely on our providers' Standard Contractual Clauses or EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework certification.

### 6.3 Age Requirement

The Services are for people aged **16 and older**. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe someone under 16 has an account, contact us and we will close it and delete its personal information.

### 6.4 Changes to This Policy

The date at the top shows when this policy last changed. For a material change, we notify you in the Services or by email before it takes effect.
