## How IxStats is laid out

Every page (except the full-screen maps) has two pieces of navigation:

- **The sidebar** (on a wide screen) or **the tab bar** (on a phone or narrow window) takes you between the main areas of the site.
- **Halo**, the floating bar at the top centre of the page, is for search, notifications, messages and quick settings.

## The sidebar

The sidebar on the left lists the areas ("apps") of IxStats. Selecting one opens it and shows its sections underneath:

| App | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | [Dashboard](/dashboard), [Messages](/messages), ThinkTanks, ThinkPages, and What's new when there's an update |
| **MyCountry** | Your nation: Overview, Directives, Economy, Diplomacy, Politics and Defense (Defense with [Premium](/help/getting-started/premium)) |
| **Maps** | The world map |
| **Vault** | IxCredits, your daily reward, cards, collections, the marketplace, crafting and import |
| **Wiki** | The wiki, search, recent changes, categories, your watchlist and contributions, Stashes, the media repository and [Blurbs](/help/social/blurbs) |
| **Forum** | Forums, trending, new posts, search, bookmarks and new thread |
| **Realms** | [Countries](/countries), [Explore](/explore), Collections, [Leaderboards](/leaderboards) and [Realms](/realms) |
| **Labs** | Onoma, Vexel, MyLeague and MyClub |
| **Help** | These guides, What's new, and the Ixnay Discord |
| **Settings** | Every [settings panel](/help/getting-started/settings) |

Some apps only appear when they apply to you. Home, MyCountry, Vault, Labs and Settings need you to be signed in; Admin is for admins only. Admins can also hide Maps, Labs or Help for everyone; Labs stays visible to admins and players with Labs access.

The collapse button at the bottom of the sidebar shrinks it to a strip of icons; select an icon to see that app's sections. Your choice is remembered. A dot on an app means it has something new, and a marker on the Vault shows when your daily reward is ready.

Your **account** sits at the bottom of the sidebar: your nation, your passport and wiki profile, your nation's public page, IxnayID connections and sign out. If you own more than one nation, choose which one you're playing as there.

## The tab bar on phones

On a narrow screen the sidebar becomes a bar at the bottom with four apps and **More**. **More** opens a sheet with the current app's sections, every other app and your account.

## Halo

Halo shows the [IxTime](/help/getting-started/ixtime) clock and a greeting, and expands into different views:

| Shortcut | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `Ctrl` + `K` (`Cmd` + `K` on Mac) | Open or close **search** |
| `Ctrl` + `N` (`Cmd` + `N`) | Open **notifications and messages** |
| `Ctrl` + `,` (`Cmd` + `,`) | Open **quick settings** |
| `Esc` | Clear the search, or close the view |

Shortcuts don't fire while you're typing in a text box, except `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + `K` in Halo's own search box.

Some areas add their own controls to Halo. For example, MyCountry adds your nation's key figures and actions, the wiki adds reading progress and the article narrator, and the Country Builder adds your build progress. On the wiki, `Tab` opens and closes the wiki view.

On the [world map](/maps), Halo is replaced by the map's own floating bar.

### Search

Search finds countries, pages and features, wiki articles and commands. Press `Tab` to cycle the filter: all, countries, commands, features, wiki. Typing a command such as "Toggle Dark/Light Theme", "Toggle Audio" or "Mark All Notifications Read" runs it straight away. You can turn wiki results off with **Wiki results in search** in [Settings](/help/getting-started/settings#wikios).

### Notifications and messages

The bell shows your unread count, and the browser tab shows it too, as "(3) IxStats". The tray has two tabs:

- **Notifications**: the latest eight, grouped into Recent, Earlier today, This week and Earlier. Selecting one marks it read and opens what it's about; you can also dismiss it.
- **Messages**: your eight newest conversations. Select one to open it in [Messages](/messages).

**Read all** marks every notification and message read. Notifications appear only inside IxStats; there are no emails or push alerts.

### Quick settings

Theme, compact mode and sound, plus the wiki reader options and image appearance when you're on the wiki, and a link to the full [Settings](/settings).
