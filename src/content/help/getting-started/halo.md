## How IxStates is laid out

Every page (except the full-screen maps) has two pieces of navigation:

- **The sidebar** (on a wide screen) or **the tab bar** (on a phone or narrow window) takes you between the main areas of the site.
- **Halo**, the floating bar at the top centre of the page, is for search, notifications, messages and quick settings.

## The sidebar

The sidebar on the left lists the areas ("apps") of IxStates. Selecting one opens it and shows its sections underneath:

| App            | What's in it                                                                                                                                                               |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Home**       | [Home](/dashboard), [Messages](/messages), and [What's new](/changelog) when an update arrives                                                                             |
| **MyCountry**  | [Your nation](/mycountry), where the page ribbon switches between Overview, Directives, Economy, Diplomacy, Politics, and [Premium](/help/getting-started/premium) Defense |
| **Maps**       | The [world map](/maps)                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Vault**      | [Wallet](/vault), Daily reward, Store, Achievements, Lorewards, Leaderboards, and Cards (My cards, Sets, Market, Explore, and Import deck)                                 |
| **Wiki**       | [Main page](/wiki), Search, Recent changes, Categories, Repository, [Stash](/help/wiki/stash), Lorewards, and Utilities                                                    |
| **ThinkPages** | [Forums](/thinkpages), [ThinkTanks](/help/social/thinktanks), Accounts, and Moderation (for moderators)                                                                    |
| **Realms**     | [My realm](/countries) and [Explore](/realms) (plus Realm admin for administrators)                                                                                        |
| **Labs**       | Onoma, Vexel, [Exchange](/exchange), MyLeague, and MyClub                                                                                                                  |
| **Help**       | These guides, [What's new](/changelog), and the Ixnay Discord                                                                                                              |
| **Settings**   | Every [settings panel](/help/getting-started/settings): Profile, Preferences, and Vault                                                                                    |

Some apps only appear when they apply to you. Home, MyCountry, Vault, Labs, and Settings need you to be signed in; Admin is for administrators only. ThinkPages shows its section rows while you are inside ThinkPages. Administrators can also hide Wiki, Maps, Labs, or Help sitewide; Labs stays visible to administrators and members with Labs access.

The collapse button at the bottom of the sidebar shrinks it to a strip of icons; select an icon to see that app's sections. Your choice is remembered. A dot on an app means it has something new, and a marker on Vault shows when your daily reward is ready.

Your **account** sits at the bottom of the sidebar: your nation (its public page), your **Passport**, your [Stash](/help/wiki/stash), **Passport settings** (IxnayID & Passport), and sign out. If you own more than one nation, choose which one you're playing as there.

## The tab bar on phones

On a narrow screen the sidebar becomes a bar at the bottom with four apps and **More**. **More** opens a sheet with the current app's sections, every other app, and your account.

## Halo

Halo shows the [IxTime](/help/getting-started/ixtime) clock and a greeting, and expands into different views:

| Shortcut                          | What it does                                                               |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Ctrl` + `K` (`Cmd` + `K` on Mac) | Open or close **search**                                                   |
| `Ctrl` + `,` (`Cmd` + `,`)        | Open or close **quick settings**                                           |
| `Esc`                             | Clear the search box, or close the open view                               |
| `Up` / `Down` and `Enter`         | Highlight a search result and open or run it                               |
| `Left` / `Right`                  | Switch search filters (`All`, `Wiki`, `Countries`, `Commands`, `Features`) |

Global shortcuts use modifier keys (plus `Esc` when Halo is open) and never get in the way while you're typing in a text box; `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + `K` also works inside Halo's own search box.

Some areas add their own controls to Halo. Selecting the greeting opens your **Account** panel, which switches between **Nation** (your nation's poster, vitality strip, **Edit** menu for the Country Editor and Map Editor, **Share**, **Manage** tiles, and **Open in** links) and **Passport** (your compact Passport card, **Play as** nation switcher when you hold multiple nations, saved Wiki drafts, shortcuts to Vault, Messages, and Settings, and sign out). MyCountry pages add quick statecraft actions, the Wiki adds reading progress, section jumps, and the article narrator, live sports matches show a live scoreboard, and founding a nation or using the Country Editor adds **Jump to section**.

On the [world map](/maps), Halo is replaced by the map's own floating bar.

### Search

Search finds open realms, countries, commands, features, Wiki articles, and actions on the current page (badged **On this page**, such as jumping to a Country Editor section or Wiki heading, declaring a directive in MyCountry, or opening a live sports match). Use the filter buttons (`All`, `Wiki`, `Countries`, `Commands`, or `Features`) or the `Left` and `Right` arrow keys on them to narrow results, move through the list with the `Up` and `Down` arrow keys, and press `Enter` (or select a row) to open a page or run a command such as "Toggle dark or light theme", "Toggle sound effects", or "Mark all notifications read". When you open Search on the Wiki, **Wiki results in search** in [Settings](/help/getting-started/settings#wikios) (also called **Quick search** in Halo's Wiki settings) controls whether Search starts on the **Wiki** filter or **All**.

### Notifications and messages

The bell shows your unread count, and the browser tab shows it too, as "(3) IxStates". The tray has two tabs:

- **Notifications**: the latest eight, grouped into Recent, Earlier today, This week, and Earlier. Selecting one marks it read and opens what it's about; you can also dismiss it. When your nation has pending national issues, a pinned row at the top takes you straight to Executive in [MyCountry](/mycountry/executive).
- **Messages**: your eight newest conversations. Select one to open it in [Messages](/messages).

**Read all** marks every notification and message read. Notifications appear only inside IxStates; there are no emails or push alerts.

### Quick settings

Theme, compact mode, and sound, plus the Wiki reader options and image appearance when you're on the Wiki, and a link to the full [Settings](/settings).
