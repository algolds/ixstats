## Three ways to get a nation

You need a nation to use [MyCountry](/mycountry). There are three ways to get one, and all of them start from [Setup](/setup) (you're sent there after signing in if you don't have a nation yet) or directly from [Found a nation](/builder):

- **Found a new nation** by choosing the kind of nation you have in mind, then naming it.
- **Import a nation** from an IIWiki or AltHistory Wiki country page; the import fills in what it finds and suggests a starting kind.
- **Claim an existing nation** that is already on the roster, for example your nation from the wiki. Choose **Link to Existing Country** in Setup, or use the claim list on a [realm page](/help/world/realms).

## Found a new nation

1. Open [Found a nation](/builder). If more than one realm has room for you, first pick the realm.
2. **Start.** Pick the kind of nation closest to what you have in mind, such as a wealthy welfare state or a skilled industrial economy. Each sets a starting size, wealth and government. **Details** on a card says what it fills in and which real country it is calibrated on. Or choose **Start blank**.
3. **Identity.** Only the name is required. Add a flag, capital, language, currency and motto if you like; **More details** holds the rest.
4. **Government.** Pick a form of government, such as a parliamentary republic or a constitutional monarchy. It sets your leaders' titles and a working set of institutions. **Customise institutions** lets you rename them, apply a policy preset or choose your own (see [Building Your Government](/help/government/atomic)).
5. **Economy.** Choose how big and how wealthy your nation is. Each choice has **Enter exact figure** if you have a number in mind.
6. **Review.** Check everything, then choose **Found nation** and confirm.

The **Nation** button in the footer shows your nation's figures as you go: GDP, budget balance, stability, government effectiveness and expected growth. They come from the same calculation that founding uses.

Your draft saves as you go, so you can leave and come back, even on another device. Budget, departments, taxes, sectors and jobs are not chosen here: they are generated from your choices so the budget starts balanced, and you adjust them in MyCountry.

## Import from a wiki

1. On the Start step, choose **Import from the wiki**.
2. Pick **IIWiki** or **AltHistory**, search for your country's page and select it.
3. Check what was found (name, flag, capital, population, wealth, government, language, currency and motto) and untick anything you don't want.
4. The Start step then marks the closest kind of nation as **Suggested**. Carry on from there.

## Claim an existing nation

1. Go to [Setup](/setup) and choose **Link to Existing Country**, or open a realm from the [realm directory](/realms) and use its claim list.
2. Search for the nation and submit a claim.
3. A moderator reviews the claim. If you have verified the wiki account that created the nation's wiki page, the claim is approved at once. Verify your wiki account in [Settings → IxnayID & Passport](/settings) first; see [IxnayID & Your Passport](/help/getting-started/ixnayid).

Each realm limits how many nations one account can own there (usually one; Premium accounts can hold more where the realm allows). Founding and claims tell you if you are at the limit.

## After your nation is founded

- A welcome screen opens, then [MyCountry](/mycountry), with a **First steps** list: review your budget, set your tax policy, appoint your cabinet, write your wiki page and place your capital on the map. Each step ticks itself once you've done it. Start with the [MyCountry overview](/help/mycountry/overview).
- Your nation appears in [Explore](/countries), on the [leaderboards](/leaderboards) and on [the map](/maps) once it has territory.
- You can change your name, identity, government and size later in the [Country Editor](/help/mycountry/editor).

## Common questions

**Can I have more than one nation?** Yes, within each realm's limit. Switch the nation you're playing as from the menu under your avatar.

**Does the kind of nation lock anything in?** No. It only sets your starting figures and government; you can change them before you found and later in the editor.

**My claim is pending. What now?** Wait for a moderator, or verify your wiki account and claim again for an instant approval if you created that nation's wiki page.
