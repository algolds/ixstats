## The Top Menu

- **The top bar** is your home row. Once you’re signed in, it gets you to your [Dashboard](/dashboard), Explore, [MyCountry](/mycountry), the [world map](/maps), the Forum, the Wiki, your [Cards & Vault](/vault), Labs, and this [Help Center](/help). The menu adjusts to who you are and what you can do.
- **The MyCountry command bar** jumps you straight into any part of your nation — Overview, Diplomacy, Defense, Politics, and Economy & Budget — plus your Profile, the map Editor, and **Declare a Directive**.
- **Your home page** gathers quick links, alerts, and live cards so you can pick right back up where you left off.

## Shortcuts Worth Knowing

> **Get around faster**
>
> - **Search anything, instantly.** Press `Ctrl` + `K` (or `Cmd` + `K` on a Mac) to open the command palette and jump anywhere on the platform.
> - Inside MyCountry, use the command bar to move between sections like [Overview](/help/mycountry/overview), [Diplomacy](/help/mycountry/diplomacy), [Defense](/help/mycountry/defense), and [Politics](/help/mycountry/politics), or open [Declare a Directive](/help/mycountry/executive).
> - Helpful nudges and alerts can appear anywhere in MyCountry when something needs your attention — a decision to make, or an opportunity worth a look.

## Light & Dark

- Switch between light, dark, or your system setting in **Settings → Appearance & Theme**.
- Your choice is remembered, so it stays the way you like it every visit.
- Different areas have their own signature colors — gold for [MyCountry](/help/mycountry/overview), blue for the wider world — and they look great in both modes.
