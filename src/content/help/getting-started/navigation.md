## The Top Menu

- **The top bar** is your home row. Once you’re signed in, it gets you to [MyCountry](/mycountry), the [Country Builder](/builder), your [dashboards](/dashboard), the [community](/thinkpages), and this [Help Center](/help). The menu adjusts to who you are and what you can do.
- **The MyCountry menu** jumps you straight into any part of your nation — Overview, Executive, Diplomacy, Intelligence, Defense, Politics, and the Map Editor.
- **Your home page** gathers quick links, alerts, and live cards so you can pick right back up where you left off.

## Shortcuts Worth Knowing

> **Get around faster**
>
> - **Search anything, instantly.** Press `Ctrl` + `K` (or `Cmd` + `K` on a Mac) to open the command palette and jump anywhere on the platform.
> - Inside MyCountry, use the sidebar to move between sections like [Overview](/help/mycountry/overview), [Executive](/help/mycountry/executive), [Intelligence](/help/mycountry/intelligence), [Diplomacy](/help/mycountry/diplomacy), and [Defense](/help/mycountry/defense).
> - Helpful nudges and alerts can appear anywhere in MyCountry when something needs your attention — a decision to make, or an opportunity worth a look.

## Light & Dark

- Switch between light and dark from your profile menu in the top-right corner.
- Your choice is remembered, so it stays the way you like it every visit.
- Different areas have their own signature colors — gold for [MyCountry](/help/mycountry/overview), blue for the wider world — and they look great in both modes.
