## What Premium is

MyCountry Premium is a membership tier on your account. Most of IxStats is free: building and running a nation, directives, issues, economy, politics, diplomacy, maps, the wiki, the feed, messages and the Vault. Premium unlocks a few extra MyCountry sections and a higher nation limit.

> [!WARNING]
> **There is no way to buy Premium in the app yet.** Membership is set by platform admins at `/admin/membership`. If you think you should have it, ask an admin.

## What Premium unlocks today

- **Defense.** The full [Defense & Security](/help/mycountry/defense) section: creating branches and assets, planning operations, conflicts and resolving security events. Without Premium, Defense opens as a read-only preview with an upgrade banner.
- **The MyCountry map-editor section.** Free players can still edit their territory on [/maps](/maps); see [Editing Your Territory](/help/mycountry/map-editor).
- **More nations per realm.** Free accounts can hold 1 nation per realm; Premium accounts up to 5, where the realm allows it (each realm sets its own cap, usually 1).

## What Premium does not do (yet)

Some older descriptions of Premium mention an intelligence dashboard, higher rate limits, more ThinkPages accounts and export quotas. None of these exist today: every account can have up to 25 ThinkPages personas, and rate limits are the same for everyone. See [Intelligence & Insights](/help/mycountry/intelligence) for what analysis tools are available to all players.
