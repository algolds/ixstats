## What IxnayID is

IxnayID is your IxStats account. One sign-in covers IxStats, the wiki reader and editor, the forum pages, IxMaps and the Vault. It also gives you a public **passport**: a profile page that gathers your nations, achievements, cards, wiki work and activity in one place. You manage IxnayID in [Settings → IxnayID & Passport](/settings), or from **IxnayID Connections** in the user menu.

## Your passport

Your passport lives at `/@yourname` (also `/id/yourname`). Anyone can view it, signed in or not. It has five tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Your featured realm, a showcase (achievements and ribbons, your top cards, Lorewards standing) and affiliations |
| Realms | Every realm you belong to and the nations you hold |
| Work | Your wiki pages and edits, Onoma languages, MyLeague clubs and directives |
| Vault | Collector level, deck value and your top cards: your pinned showcase when it is public, otherwise your six most valuable cards |
| History | Your activity across the platform |

A player's `User:` page in the wiki opens their passport, and author links in the wiki open its Work tab (`/@handle?tab=work`).

## Edit your passport and privacy

1. Open your own passport and choose **Edit passport**. The card flips to its settings side.
2. Switch sections on or off: Achievements, Civic accolades ([Lorewards](/help/wiki/lorewards)), Focus, Forum activity, IxCredits (your whole Vault) and Activity history. Hidden sections are hidden from everyone, including you, so you see what visitors see.
3. Write a signature (up to 60 characters) and pin up to three **signature ribbons** from achievements you've unlocked. See [Ribbons & Your Showcase](/help/gameplay/ribbons).
4. Choose **Done** to save.

Your Realms and Work tabs are always public.

## Your handle in WikiOS

Your passport handle is the `@name` in your passport address. WikiOS signs every edit you make with it, and your `User:` page (`User:yourhandle`) opens your passport.

- **No handle yet?** The first time you save in WikiOS, it asks you to pick one, with a suggestion filled in. Choose **Save and continue** and your edit goes on.
- A handle is 3 to 24 lowercase letters, numbers or underscores. Picking your first one is free; after that you can change it once, in [Settings](/settings) → **IxnayID & Passport** → **Passport handle**.
- A handle can't be the same as an old IxWiki name another player holds.
- Bots log in to the wiki with a bot password as `yourhandle@AppName`. Create bot passwords at [Special:BotPasswords](/util/botpasswords).

## Claim your old IxWiki names

If you edited the classic wiki (IxWiki) under a user name, ask for that name to join your account. An administrator checks each name before it does. New players don't need one: WikiOS works with your handle alone.

1. Open [Settings](/settings) → **IxnayID & Passport** → **Old IxWiki names**.
2. Enter the wiki user name. If you like, say how we can tell it's yours: your alts, renames or pages you made.
3. Choose **Ask to add**. The name shows "Waiting for review" until an administrator decides. It then reads "Approved" or "Not approved", sometimes with a note.

You can claim several names (alts and renames), but each name belongs to one account. Once a name is approved:

- its past edits count as yours: in your contributions, toward the 10 edits that unlock uploads and the other autoconfirmed rights, and in achievements;
- its `User:` page opens your passport;
- edits you make in WikiOS reach the classic wiki under that name;
- classic-wiki [Lorewards](/help/wiki/lorewards) won under it count for you;
- claims for nations whose IxWiki page it created are approved at once;
- bots set up under it keep logging in.

**Show or hide a name.** Each name has a switch. Turned off, the name shows nowhere other players look (your passport, your wiki pages and bylines), but it still counts for you. If you had hidden your wiki name in your privacy settings before, your names start hidden: turn on each one you want shown.

An administrator can remove a name later if it turns out not to be yours. Its edits then stop counting for you; Lorewards already paid stay paid.

## Link your sister wiki accounts

Linking proves you own an account on IIWiki or AltHistory Wiki. In a realm hosted on that wiki, it makes claims for nations whose wiki page you created approve instantly.

1. Open [Settings](/settings) → **IxnayID & Passport** → **Linked accounts** and choose **Manage**.
2. Under **Sister wikis**, next to IIWiki or AltHistory, enter your wiki username and choose **Get code**.
3. Paste the code anywhere on your user page on that wiki and save it while logged in as that user.
4. Come back and choose **Verify**. The code works for 24 hours; if it expires, choose **New code**.

## Old forum posts

The old forum (forum.ixwiki.com) is retired, so forum accounts are no longer linked here. **Linked accounts** shows your old forum account read-only: "Imported as" your old forum name when it is linked, or "No old-forum account". Linking is managed by staff: to have your old forum posts attributed to you, ask in a thread in the forum's Reports category with your old forum username. Staff link it in the admin users panel, and your imported posts show your name straight away. See [ThinkPages (the forum)](/help/social/forum#getting-your-posts-attributed).

## Link Discord

Discord is linked by signing in to your account with Discord. Once linked, it shows as Connected and you can unlink it from the same row.

## Keeping your account safe

- Only paste verification codes on your own user page or profile.
- Nobody from staff will ask you for a code or password.
- Use **Unlink** if a linked account is wrong, then link it again.
