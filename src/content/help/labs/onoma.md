## What Onoma is

[Onoma](/labs/onoma) is the Labs tool for names and languages. Use it to generate names for people, places and groups in your nation, or go further and design the sounds, words, grammar and writing of a language. It's in the sidebar under **Labs** when Labs is switched on for you, and you can also find it with `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + `K`.

Generating names works without signing in. Saving names, dictionaries and language packs needs a signed-in account.

## The three workspaces

Onoma is split into three workspaces, chosen at the top of the page.

### Create

- **Sandbox**: quick name generation in one place. Pick a style, set how many names you want, and generate.
- **Places**, **People**, **Factions** and **Culture**: generators for one kind of name each, with their own sub-types (for example, kinds of places).

Each result shows the name, its pronunciation (IPA) and how it's built. Select the speaker icon to hear it.

### Studio

- **Workshop**: train a name model on a set of example names, and see how it links sounds together.
- **Path visualizer**: explore the paths the model takes when it builds a name.
- **Name sets**: ready-made sets of example names to start from.
- **Sound shifts**: rules for how sounds change over time, with a before-and-after view, plus **loanwords** (how words borrowed from another language are adapted).

### Explore

- **Acoustics & IPA**: your language's sounds on a vowel chart and spectrum, and a comparison of sound profiles.
- **Grammar & roots**: word roots and how words are derived from them, and a sentence builder with word order, affixes and a dictionary.
- **Writing systems**: design an alphabet. Draw glyphs, map them to sounds, and try writing text.
- **Community packs**: language packs other players have shared. You can rate a pack or copy (fork) it to work on your own version.

## Saving your work

- Use **Stash** (top right) to keep names you like and your custom dictionaries. Saved names go to your name bank.
- You can make a dictionary public so other players can find and copy it.
- Some Studio work (custom name overrides, sound rules and word definitions) is kept in this browser only. To move it to another device or keep a backup, use **Settings** (top right) → the data manager to back it up and restore it.

## Pronunciation and voices

Names are read aloud with a natural voice when the admins have switched it on; otherwise your browser's built-in voice is used. **Settings** has voice options and a sandbox for trying how a word sounds.

## Help inside Onoma

The **Help** button in Onoma's header opens guided walkthroughs of each workspace.

## Good to know

- Onoma is a Labs tool: it changes more often than the rest of IxStats.
- Names you make aren't attached to your nation automatically. Copy them into your [Country Editor](/help/mycountry/editor), the [wiki](/help/wiki/wikios) or wherever you need them.
