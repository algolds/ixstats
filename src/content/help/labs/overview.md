## What Labs is

Labs holds experimental tools that sit alongside the main game. They work, but they change more often than the rest of IxStats and some parts are unfinished. **Labs** in the sidebar lists Onoma, Vexel, MyLeague and MyClub. You need to be signed in, and Labs must be switched on: admins can hide it for everyone, but admins and players with Labs access always see it. Every Labs page can also be opened directly by address, or found with `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + `K`.

## Onoma (languages and names)

[Onoma](/labs/onoma) builds languages and names for your nation.

- **Generate names** for people and places from a language style, with pronunciation read aloud (the higher-quality voice is limited to some accounts; everyone else gets the browser's voice).
- **Design a language:** sounds (phonology), word structure, spelling, sound changes over time, loanwords and simple sentence building.
- **Writing systems:** design an alphabet: draw glyphs, map them to sounds and try writing text (in Onoma's Explore workspace).
- The **glyph catalog** ([/labs/onoma/glyphs](/labs/onoma/glyphs)) is a reference gallery of the icons Onoma itself uses; it isn't an editor.
- **Save** names and dictionaries to your [Stash](/help/wiki/stash) name bank, and share language packs with others.
- Languages you make appear on your [passport's](/help/getting-started/ixnayid) Work tab.

The full guide is [Onoma](/help/labs/onoma).

## MyLeague and MyClub (sports)

- [MyLeague](/myleague) runs simulated sports leagues across seven sports: soccer, Formula 1, hockey, boxing, basketball, baseball and American football. A league has standings, a schedule, brackets or races, a draft, teams and history.
- [MyClub](/myclub) is where you manage a team: roster, tactics, transfers, management and history.
- Matches are simulated; each one can be replayed from its stored result.

The full guide is [MyLeague & MyClub](/help/labs/myleague-myclub).

## Vexel (flags and heraldry)

[Vexel](/labs/vexel), listed under Labs in the sidebar, is a flag and coat-of-arms designer with a generator and a registry of designs. Attaching a Vexel design to your country doesn't work yet, so export your design and add it in the [Country Editor](/help/mycountry/editor) instead. The full guide is [Vexel](/help/labs/vexel).

## Map pipeline

[/labs/map-pipeline](/labs/map-pipeline) is an experimental world-map generator (terrain, climate, rivers). Results aren't saved and don't create nations, and the regional profiles and resources it shows are labelled as sample data; it's a preview of future realm generation.
