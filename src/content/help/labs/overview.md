## What Labs is

Labs holds experimental tools that sit alongside the main game. They work, but they change more often than the rest of IxStats and some parts are unfinished. The **Labs** menu in the top bar lists MyLeague and Onoma when Labs is switched on for your account (admins control who sees it). Every Labs page can also be opened directly by address, or found with `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + `K`.

## Onoma (languages and names)

[Onoma](/labs/onoma) builds languages and names for your nation.

- **Generate names** for people and places from a language style, with pronunciation read aloud (the higher-quality voice is limited to some accounts; everyone else gets the browser's voice).
- **Design a language:** sounds (phonology), word structure, spelling, sound changes over time, loanwords and simple sentence building.
- **Writing systems:** design an alphabet in the glyph editor ([/labs/onoma/glyphs](/labs/onoma/glyphs)).
- **Save** names and dictionaries to your [Stash](/help/wiki/stash) name bank, and share language packs with others.
- Languages you make appear on your [passport's](/help/getting-started/ixnayid) Work tab.

## MyLeague and MyClub (sports)

- [MyLeague](/myleague) runs simulated sports leagues across seven sports: soccer, Formula 1, hockey, boxing, basketball, baseball and American football. A league has standings, a schedule, brackets or races, a draft, teams and history.
- [MyClub](/myclub) is where you manage a team: roster, tactics, transfers, management and history.
- Matches are simulated; each one can be replayed from its stored result.

## Vexel (flags and heraldry)

[Vexel](/labs/vexel) is a flag and coat-of-arms designer with a generator and a registry of designs. It isn't in the Labs menu yet. Attaching a Vexel design to your country isn't reliable yet, so export your design and set your flag in the [Country Editor](/help/mycountry/editor) instead.

## Map pipeline

[/labs/map-pipeline](/labs/map-pipeline) is an experimental world-map generator (terrain, climate, rivers). Results aren't saved and don't create nations; it's a preview of future realm generation.
