## What they are

[MyLeague](/myleague) and [MyClub](/myclub) are IxStats' sports simulation, part of [Labs](/help/labs/overview). **MyLeague** is the league side: competitions, schedules, standings and champions. **MyClub** is the owner side: the teams you run. Both need you to be signed in, and both are in the sidebar under **Labs** when Labs is switched on for you.

Seven sports are available: soccer, American football, ice hockey, basketball, baseball, Formula 1 and boxing. Matches are simulated, not played live, and each result can be replayed from its stored simulation.

## MyLeague

The [MyLeague lobby](/myleague) shows a featured league and every other league, with filters by sport and status (active, paused, completed, archived) and a search box. Leagues made by players are marked as custom.

### Creating a league

Choose **Create League**, pick a sport, set it up and confirm. The league is created with generated teams and rosters.

### Inside a league

A league page has tabs:

- **Overview**: the current season, team count, progress and reigning champion.
- **Standings**: the table, with promotion and relegation zones if the league uses them.
- **Schedule**: match cards by match day.
- **Bracket** (boxing) or **Race Results** (Formula 1).
- **Draft**: shown when the season has draft picks.
- **Teams**: every team, filterable by managed or unclaimed.
- **History**: past champions, all-time records and each past season.

Open any result to see the box score, commentary and analysis.

### Running the season

Only the league's creator (and the site's system owners) can run a league. Other players may see the simulate buttons, but the server refuses their presses. The creator's **League controls** panel sits beside the league:

1. **Start Season 1** builds the schedule.
2. **Simulate** in the league header (or the `Space` key), or **Simulate Round** on the Overview and Schedule tabs, plays the next round. **Simulate rest of season** plays everything left, including playoffs, brackets and races.
3. When a season is complete, **Start Season 2** (and so on) develops players, runs a draft and starts the next season.

The server can also play in-progress seasons automatically as their match days fall due on the [IxTime](/help/getting-started/ixtime) clock. A league its commissioner has paused stays where it is.

## MyClub

[MyClub](/myclub) lists the teams you own. If you don't own one yet, it points you to MyLeague.

### Claiming a team

Open an unclaimed team from a league's **Teams** tab and choose **Claim**. You need:

- a **MyClub Team License Token** from the Vault Store (5,000 IxCredits), and
- for an official (canonical) league, also a **MyLeague Franchise Pass** (2,500 IxCredits).

See [IxCredits](/help/vault/ixcredits) for how to earn credits. A team someone else owns can't be claimed.

### Managing your club

A team page has six sections:

| Section | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Follow the live match ticker, see your record and upcoming matches, and run a team training session |
| **Roster** | See each player's ratings, age and career stage; train a player; put a player on the transfer list |
| **Tactics** | Pick one of seven tactical styles, set attack focus and intensity, and save a starting lineup and captain |
| **Transfers** | Search players across leagues, bid on listed players, and accept or reject bids for yours |
| **Management** | Set ticket prices, expand the stadium, choose a sponsor, and collect match-day revenue |
| **History** | Titles, all-time record and season-by-season finishes |

### Money

- **Sovereigns (₷)** are the sports currency, kept apart from IxCredits. Training a player costs ₷25, a team session ₷100, and stadium expansion and transfer bids are paid in Sovereigns too. Your Sovereign wallet starts with a balance set by the admins. You can't convert IxCredits into Sovereigns yet.
- **Match revenue** goes into your club's budget when you collect it in **Management**: ticket sales and your sponsor's base fee for each completed home match, plus the sponsor's bonus for each win. Each match pays once.
