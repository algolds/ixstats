## What Vexel is

[Vexel](/labs/vexel) is the Labs studio for heraldry: coats of arms for your nation, its people, institutions and dynasties. You build a design from parts (the field, divisions, ordinaries and charges, each in a heraldic colour), and Vexel draws the shield and writes its **blazon**, the formal heraldic description. It's in the sidebar under **Labs** when Labs is switched on for you.

## Designing arms

1. Open [the studio](/labs/vexel).
2. Build the design in the panels: **layers** (what's on the shield, in order), **properties** (colours and settings of the selected part) and the **charge library** (animals, objects, crowns and other symbols to place).
3. Watch the **blazon** update as you go.
4. Check the **validation** panel. It warns when a design breaks the traditional colour rules (for example, a colour placed on another colour). These are warnings only; you can still save.

Only the shield is drawn today. Crests, helmets, supporters and mottos aren't shown yet.

## Saving and publishing

- Give the design a title, choose who it's for (**Character**, **Country**, **Institution** or **Dynasty**), and choose **Save changes**. You need to be signed in. Each save keeps a revision you can go back to.
- Choose **Publish** to list the design in the public [registry](/labs/vexel/registry). **Unpublish** takes it out again.

## Exporting

**Export** downloads the shield as an SVG (vector) file, or as a PNG at 256 × 256 or 1024 × 1024 pixels. If your design uses charges from Wikimedia Commons, credit their authors as their licences require.

## Using your arms on your nation

The **Attach to map** button (shown for a saved Country design) doesn't work yet. It needs a stored image of the design, and Vexel doesn't make one yet, so attaching gives an error and your nation's coat of arms stays as it was.

For now, export the design and add the image to your nation yourself: the coat of arms in the [Country Editor](/help/mycountry/editor), or your flag in [Settings](/help/getting-started/settings).

## Ideas and inspiration

- The [generator gallery](/labs/vexel/generate) makes random designs. You can steer it by culture, religion and government type.
- The [registry](/labs/vexel/registry) shows everyone's published arms. Open one to see it in full with its blazon.
