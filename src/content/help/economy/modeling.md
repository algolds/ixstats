## What the modeling page is

The modeling page is a sandbox for projecting a country's economy under different assumptions. Open it at `/countries/<country>/modeling`, or from a country profile's tools (**Economic Modeling**). You need to be signed in. Running scenarios never changes the country's actual figures.

## How to run a scenario

1. Open the modeling page for your nation (or any other).
2. **Parameters:** set growth, inflation, unemployment, interest rates and similar assumptions.
3. **Sectors:** adjust the mix of your economy.
4. **Policies:** try policy levers such as tax and spending changes.
5. **Projections:** choose how many years ahead to project (1 to 50) and read the resulting GDP, debt, unemployment and revenue paths, compared with the current baseline.

The model health score at the bottom summarises how sustainable the scenario looks.

## Saving

**Save Economic Model** stores your scenario's parameters on your nation's economic profile so you can come back to them. It doesn't change your live GDP, growth or tier; those follow the rules in [How Your Economy Is Calculated](/help/economy/calculations).

## Good to know

- The sandbox uses its own simplified maths, so its projections won't exactly match how MyCountry projects your nation.
- Test extreme cases (a deep recession, very high growth) to see where your assumptions break.
- To change your real economy, use [directives](/help/mycountry/executive) and [national issues](/help/gameplay/national-issues).
