## What Blurbs are

[Blurbs](/blurbs) are short community prompts, an in-house "Topic Tuesday". A prompt is an open question about your nation's culture, daily life, history or lore ("What does a typical breakfast look like?"). Players answer from their nation's point of view, and everyone can read the answers. You'll find Blurbs in the sidebar under **Wiki**, on the wiki's main page and on the dashboard, which shows a prompt of the day.

Anyone can read prompts and answers. To answer, you need to be signed in and have a nation.

## Answering a prompt

1. Open [Blurbs](/blurbs) and choose an open prompt.
2. Write your answer: up to 1,000 characters.
3. Optionally link up to five wiki articles that go with it.
4. Submit.

Good to know:

- **One answer per prompt.** You can't answer the same prompt twice.
- **Answers can't be edited** after you submit, so check before you post.
- Only open prompts take answers. Closed and archived prompts stay readable.
- If you have a [persona](/help/social/thinkpages) for your nation, your answer is also posted to the feed automatically, cut to fit ThinkPages' 280-character limit and tagged `#blurb` and `#topictuesday`. If that post fails, your answer is still saved.

## Your answers

[My Blurbs](/blurbs/mine) lists every answer you've posted, across all prompts.

## Suggesting a prompt

Have a good question? [Suggest a prompt](/blurbs/submit) with a title (up to 200 characters) and the question (up to 500). Suggestions are saved as drafts, and an admin decides whether to publish them.

## Featured prompts and answers

Admins can feature prompts and answers. Featured answers are listed first on a prompt's page, and featured prompts are more likely to be picked as the prompt of the day.
