## What the activity feed is

The activity feed is the running record of what's happening across IxStats: posts, nation milestones, wiki writing and forum discussion, newest first. You'll find it on your [Dashboard](/dashboard), and as a full page with filters at [/feed](/feed). Anyone can read it. Posting is covered in [ThinkPages & the Feed](/help/social/thinkpages).

## What appears in it

| Kind | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Posts | Public [ThinkPages](/help/social/thinkpages) posts, including Blurbs answers |
| Nation activity | An achievement unlocked by a player with a nation, an embassy opened, a border change, a new point of interest on the map |
| Community | New polls, names shared from Onoma |
| Wiki | New wiki pages and edits, with how many bytes changed |
| Forum | New forum threads and replies |

To keep it readable, several edits in a row to the same wiki page are shown as one item, and so are repeats of the same kind of activity by the same player. The Dashboard feed refreshes about once a minute.

## The Dashboard tabs

- **All activity**: everything above.
- **Following**: posts from personas you follow and activity from nations your nation follows. If you don't follow anyone yet, it links to the list of nations.
- **Trending**: the posts getting the most reactions, replies and reposts, and the trending hashtags. It's empty when nothing qualifies.
- **Community**: wiki and forum activity only.

## Following nations and personas

- **Personas.** Open a post's author (or their profile) and choose **Follow**. By default you follow as your personal persona. See [ThinkPages](/help/social/thinkpages).
- **Nations.** The Dashboard's **Countries to explore** card has a **Follow** button for each nation. Following a nation needs a nation of your own: your nation follows theirs.

## The full feed page

[/feed](/feed) shows the same stream with more control:

- **Type:** All, Achievements, Diplomatic, Economic, Social or Platform.
- **Source:** All sources, In-Game, Platform or Social.
- Activity totals for the last day, and a **Trending now** list of hashtags.

## Hashtags

Add a hashtag to a post by typing `#` followed by letters, numbers or underscores, for example `#TradeWeek`. The hashtag is saved with the post and becomes a link in the post's text.

- **Hashtag pages.** Each hashtag has a page at `/hashtags/<tag>` listing the 20 most recent public posts with that exact tag. Tags are matched exactly, so `#TradeWeek` and `#tradeweek` have separate pages.
- **Trending topics.** Every 15 minutes, IxStats looks at hashtags on public posts from the last three days. A hashtag can trend once at least two players have used it in at least two posts. The top ten show as topic chips on the Dashboard's **Trending** tab and in **Trending now** on [/feed](/feed), with their post counts. On [/feed](/feed), an arrow marks a topic that reached a new high in the last six hours. Select a topic to open its hashtag page.

## Hiding people

Posts from accounts you've blocked or muted are left out of your feeds. Manage them in [Settings → Privacy & security](/help/getting-started/settings#privacy-security).

## Good to know

- Only public posts reach the feed, hashtag pages and trending topics.
- A nation's own activity is also listed on its country profile.
- If wiki or forum activity can't be loaded for a moment, the rest of the feed still shows.
