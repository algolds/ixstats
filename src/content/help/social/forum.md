## What the forum is

The forum is the community's discussion space, built into ThinkPages at [/thinkpages/forum](/thinkpages/forum). Anyone can read the public categories. To start a thread or reply you need to be signed in, and some places have extra rules, described below.

The forum home lists the sitewide categories first (Rules, Announcements, Reports, Find a Realm, General and Side Games), then a section for one realm with a switcher to pick another. Each realm section has its own categories: Hub for out-of-character talk, and Character Threads and Current Events for in-character writing.

## Reading

- Open a category to see its threads, pinned threads first, then the most recently active. Open a thread to read its posts, oldest first.
- Each post shows who wrote it and when. A post that has been changed after posting is marked "(edited)".
- Rules and Announcements are read-only for members: only the team starts threads there.
- Reports is private. Staff see every report thread; you see only the ones you started.
- The Staff category is for the team and does not appear for anyone else.
- You can save a thread with **Stash thread** at the top of it. Saved threads are listed with your other saved items at [/stashes](/stashes). See [Stash](/help/wiki/stash).

## Posting

1. Open a category and choose **New thread**, or open a thread and write in the reply box under it.
2. Give a new thread a title, write your post and choose **Post** (or **Reply**).
3. You can edit your own posts in a thread that is still open. Once a thread is locked or archived, replies and edits are closed to members.

Where you can post depends on the place:

- **Sitewide categories** are open to every signed-in member, except Rules and Announcements, which are for staff.
- **A realm's categories** are open to members who own a nation in that realm, and to the realm's moderators. If you have no nation there, the page says so and offers a link to claim one. A realm that has been archived is read-only.
- **Bans** stop you posting in the place they cover. The page tells you which ban applies and until when.

### In-character posts

In Side Games, Character Threads and Current Events you can post as one of your [ThinkPages personas](/help/social/thinkpages) instead of yourself. Pick it from **Post as** in the composer. A persona post shows only the persona's name and @username, never the player behind it. Other categories are out of character and always post as you.

### Action cards

Choose **Attach action** in the composer to add one of your nation's public actions to a post. It appears in the post as a card that links to the action.

## Reporting a problem

Use **Report** on another member's post, or **Report thread** at the top of a thread, to tell the moderators what is wrong. Give a reason. The moderators of that category see your report and your reason. The member you report is never told who reported them. You cannot report your own posts.

To ask the team something that is not about one post, start a thread in Reports.

## Moderation, warnings and bans

Each category is moderated by site admins, plus a realm's founder and its officers with the board power for that realm's categories, and any moderators assigned to a single category. Moderators can lock, pin, hide, archive and move threads, hide or edit posts (an edit needs a note for the moderation log), and work through reports at [Moderation](/thinkpages/mod), which only appears for people who moderate something.

- **Warnings** carry points and a reason. Points stop counting after 90 days.
- **Automatic bans:** 5 active points bring a 7-day ban from the whole forum, and 10 active points a 30-day ban.
- **Bans** can cover the whole forum, one realm's forum, or one category, for a set time or until a moderator lifts them.
- Moderators cannot warn or ban site admins, or the moderators of the place.

You are notified when you receive a warning or a ban, when a ban is lifted, and when an appeal is decided.

### Your standing and appeals

When you have a warning, a ban or an appeal on record, **Your standing** appears at the top of the [forum home](/thinkpages/forum). It lists your active points, each warning with when it expires, each ban with when it ends, and any appeals with their status and the moderator's response. Ban notices on forum pages link there too.

You can appeal each active warning and each active ban once, with a message of 10 to 4000 characters. A different moderator with authority over the place reviews it: never you, and never a moderator who issued or raised the sanction. The outcome is one of three:

- **Upheld:** the warning or ban stands.
- **Overturned:** a warning is revoked, or a ban is lifted, and any automatic ban that depended on it is recalculated.
- **Closed, it had already ended:** the warning or ban ran out before the review.

You never see which moderator issued or reviewed anything.

## Your old forum posts

The community used to run its forum on a separate site. Its threads and posts have been copied here, with their original dates.

- **Imported threads** say "Imported from the old forum." under the title, and are tagged **Imported** in thread lists.
- **Where they live:** threads from boards that matched a category here sit in that category and take replies like any other thread. Threads from every other old board sit in a category under **From the old forum** on the forum home. Those categories are read-only for members, so you can read them but not reply or start threads there. The team can still post in them.
- **Names:** a post written by someone whose old forum account was linked to an IxStats account when the posts were copied shows that member's IxStats name. Any other post keeps the name from the old forum, as plain text, with no profile behind it.
- **Getting your posts attributed:** if your old posts show your old name instead of your IxStats name, start a thread in Reports with your old forum username. Staff link the old account to your IxStats account and update the imported posts, which may take a little while. Linking is no longer something you do in Settings.
- **Old links:** links to the old forum's pages open the matching thread, post or category here when it was imported. A link that cannot be matched opens the forum home.
