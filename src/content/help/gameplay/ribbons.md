## What ribbons are

Every [achievement](/help/gameplay/achievements) you unlock is also a **ribbon**, like a service ribbon on a uniform. You don't earn ribbons separately and there's nothing to claim: unlock an achievement and its ribbon appears. Ribbons belong to your account, not to one nation.

[Lore Medals](/help/wiki/lorewards#lore-medals) and wiki article awards are listed on the Achievements page but aren't ribbons.

## Reading a ribbon

The stripe colour shows the achievement's category and the star shows its rarity. Hover over (or tap) a ribbon to see its name, description, category, rarity and when it was unlocked.

| Stripe | Category |
| --- | --- |
| Green | Economic |
| Red | Military |
| Teal | Diplomatic |
| Indigo | Government |
| Blue | Social |
| Yellow | General |

| Star | Rarity |
| --- | --- |
| Bronze | Common |
| Silver | Uncommon |
| Gold | Rare or Epic |
| White | Legendary |

## Where ribbons show

- **Your passport.** The Overview tab's showcase has an **Achievements and ribbons** panel: how many achievements you've unlocked out of the total, your points, a shelf of three ribbons, your full **ribbon rack**, and the names of a few more of your best achievements.
- **Your nations' profiles.** The header of each nation you own shows your top three ribbons, with a "+N" count for the rest. A nation with no active owner, or an owner with no ribbons, shows none.

Ribbons are ordered with your pinned ribbons first, then rarest first, then newest first.

## Pin your signature ribbons

The shelf on your passport shows your **Signature ribbons**: up to three you choose. Until you pin any, it shows your **Top ribbons** (your three rarest) instead.

1. Open your [passport](/help/getting-started/ixnayid) and choose **Edit passport**. The card flips to its settings side.
2. Under **Signature ribbons**, select up to three ribbons. The counter shows how many you've pinned (for example "2/3 pinned").
3. Select a pinned ribbon again to unpin it. Pins save as soon as you select them.

You can only pin ribbons for achievements you've unlocked.

## The rest of the passport showcase

Next to your ribbons, the showcase has:

- **Collection highlight**: your three most valuable cards, with a link to view all your cards.
- **Lorewards standing**: your rank, score, laurels and best streak. See [Lorewards](/help/wiki/lorewards).

Each panel says so when there's nothing to show yet.

## The Achievements showcase shelf

The [Achievements](/achievements) page has its own **Showcase shelf** switch in the header. When it's on, a **Rare achievements showcase** shows the unlocked achievements the fewest players have, three at a time; choose **Show top 9** to see more. This shelf is only on your Achievements page, and the switch is remembered on the device you set it on.

## Privacy

On the settings side of your passport (**Edit passport**):

- **Achievements** off hides your achievements and ribbons from your passport and your nations' profiles.
- **IxCredits** off hides your collection highlight, along with the rest of your Vault.
- **Civic accolades** off hides your Lorewards standing.

Hidden sections are hidden from everyone, including you, so you see what visitors see.
