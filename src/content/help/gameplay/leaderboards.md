## What the leaderboards show

The [Leaderboards](/leaderboards) page ranks nations on a choice of measures. Open it from **Global Leaderboards** on the [Achievements](/achievements) page, or search for it with `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + `K`. Anyone can view it.

## Measures

| Group | Measures |
| --- | --- |
| Economy & Wealth | Total GDP, GDP per capita, GDP growth, average income, government revenue, government spending |
| Demographics & Labor | Population, population density, land area, workforce, employment |
| Quality & Governance | Literacy, life expectancy |
| Achievements | Achievement score (count and rarity of unlocked achievements) |

Pick a group to narrow the list of measures, then pick a measure.

## How rankings work

- Rankings are worked out when the page loads, from the figures stored on each nation. Those are refreshed every few hours, so they can trail your live MyCountry figures a little.
- Nations are ranked within one realm, by default the realm of the nation you're playing as.
- The top three stand on the podium; everyone else is listed below with their economic and population tiers.
- Search for a nation by name, and choose how many to show (10, 25, 50 or 100).

## Related rankings

- Your nation's **World Census** card in MyCountry ranks you within your realm, region and tier on ten measures, including approval, stability and debt. See [MyCountry Overview](/help/mycountry/overview#world-census).
- [Economic Tiers](/help/economy/tiers) explains the tier shown next to each nation.
