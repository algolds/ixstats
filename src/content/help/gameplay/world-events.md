## Crises today

The crises you deal with day to day are **urgent national issues**: high-severity issues on your MyCountry agenda that can't be delegated. Handle them from their issue brief like any other [national issue](/help/gameplay/national-issues). An automatic crisis system (earthquakes, market crashes and the like, with response strategies) was designed but hasn't been built; older guides that described it now point here.

## World events

Admins (storytellers) can run **world events** such as an economic crisis, a natural disaster or a pandemic from the admin console. A world event has a severity, a duration in IxTime years and a list of affected nations.

- For each affected nation it can add an effect on that nation's economy for the event's duration. Events of severity 0.5 or more are negative.
- The effect feeds straight into the economic projection, so it shows up in your GDP and growth in MyCountry.
- When an admin ends an event, its effects stop.

You can't respond to a world event with a special action, but you can react with [directives](/help/mycountry/executive) (for example, an economy directive to offset a downturn).

## Crisis counts

The dashboard's player widget can show a count of active crises. That count comes from a separate crisis record that is only filled in demo data, so on the live platform it normally shows none.

## Crisis Hotspots on the map

The **Crisis Hotspots** overlay on [IxMaps](/help/world/maps) colours nations by an estimated crisis risk worked out from their geography (terrain, climate and similar factors). It's a risk estimate, not a list of crises that are happening.
