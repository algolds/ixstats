## What Stash is

Stash is your personal reading list and research folder. Save wiki articles, quoted passages, images and forum threads into colour-coded collections, add notes, and come back to them at [/stashes](/stashes). Your stashes are private to you.

## Saving things

- **Wiki articles:** use the stash action on an article in the [wiki](/help/wiki/wikios).
- **Quotes:** select text in an article and choose **Stash** (or **Highlight** to keep it as a highlight). Quotes link back to the section they came from.
- **Images:** stash an image from the wiki's media [Repository](/util/repository).
- **Forum threads:** stash a thread from the [forum](/help/social/forum); your saved threads are also listed in the forum's bookmarks.

Everything goes into **My Stash** unless you pick another collection.

## Organising collections

1. Open [/stashes](/stashes).
2. Create a collection, give it a name (up to 100 characters) and one of eight colours.
3. Rename, recolour, reorder or delete collections from each collection's settings menu. **My Stash** can't be deleted.

Each collection has tabs for **Articles**, **Quotes & Highlights**, **Media** and **Discussions**.

## Exporting

From a collection's settings, export it as **Markdown** (headings, quotes, image and thread links) or **JSON** (for backups or tools). Images come with a ready-made wikitext snippet you can copy.

## Good to know

- The settings menu has a "copy share link" option, but shared links don't open other people's stashes yet; stashes are private.
- Other tools also save into Stash, such as the Onoma name bank in [Labs](/help/labs/overview).
