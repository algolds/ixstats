## What the wiki is

**Wiki** in the top menu opens WikiOS at [/wiki](/wiki), IxStats' reader and editor for the community wiki (IxWiki): nation articles, history, people, organisations and everything else written about the world. Anyone can read it. Signed-in players can edit articles, discuss them and keep a watchlist. Edits made here are copied to the classic wiki in the background, and edits made on the classic wiki are pulled in.

## Reading and finding articles

- **Search** from the wiki's search box, or from anywhere with `Ctrl`/`Cmd` + `K`. Full search results are at [/util/search](/util/search).
- **Browse** by [category](/util/categories), jump to a [random article](/util/random), or see [recent changes](/util/recent-changes).
- **Other wikis.** Articles from IIWiki and AltHistory Wiki can be read in the same reader (the address ends in `?source=iiwiki` or `?source=althistory`). They're read-only here.
- **Tools** for an article: its history and diffs, what links here, and a **Watch** button that adds it to your [watchlist](/util/watchlist).
- All the tool pages are listed at [/util](/util).

## Editing an article

1. Open the article and choose edit (or go to `/wiki/<Title>/edit`).
2. Edit in the **visual** editor (blocks, tables, infoboxes, a slash menu for inserting things, and forms for templates) or switch to **source** mode for wikitext.
3. Save with a short summary. Your draft is kept in your browser while you work.

To start a new article, search for its title and choose **Create this page** on the "does not exist" page.

**Who can edit what:** signed-in players can edit ordinary articles and talk pages. You can edit your own `User:` page once you've [verified your wiki account](/help/getting-started/ixnayid#link-your-wiki-accounts). Other namespaces (templates, modules and so on) are admin-only.

## Discussing articles

- **Talk pages** at `/wiki/<Title>/talk`.
- **Margin**: comment threads pinned to parts of an article's text. Open them from the margin notes row on the article.

## Images

Images come from the wiki's media library; browse it in the [Repository](/util/repository). Uploading new files through WikiOS isn't working at the moment, so upload on the classic wiki if you need a new image.

## Your wiki profile

A player's wiki user profile now opens their [IxnayID passport](/help/getting-started/ixnayid) on the **Work** tab (`/id/@username?tab=work`), which lists their wiki pages and edits. Contribution lists are also at [/util/contributions](/util/contributions).

## Lorewards

[Lorewards](/util/lorewards) recognise wiki writing. Edits are scored on how much prose you add, how deep the edit goes, whether it's a new article, how many other articles link to it, and collaboration across nations. Daily, weekly and monthly results and streaks come from the community's Lorewards bot. Winning a Loreward pays 2,500 IxCredits (default amount), and your standing shows on your passport.

## Good to know

- Templates, Lua modules and the Main Page are still rendered by the classic wiki, so a few pages look slightly different from ordinary articles.
- Edits are copied to the classic wiki under a shared bot account; your authorship is recorded in WikiOS.
