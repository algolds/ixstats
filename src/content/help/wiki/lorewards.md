## What Lorewards are

Lorewards are the community's awards for writing on IxWiki. Every day the community's Lorewards bot picks a **winner** and a **runner-up** from that day's wiki edits, and there are also weekly, monthly and yearly winners. Weekly and monthly results record a winner; runner-up places are recorded for the daily contest. Results are published on the wiki's `IxWiki:OOL` pages. IxStats copies the approved results so your laurels, score and streak show across the platform. Nothing here needs to be claimed: you write on the wiki, and the results come to you.

## Winning a Loreward

1. Write on IxWiki, in [WikiOS](/help/wiki/wikios) or on the classic wiki.
2. The Lorewards bot reviews the day's edits and announces the winner and runner-up. Only approved results count.
3. IxStats copies new results on a schedule, so a new win can take up to a day to show.

## What a place pays

A Loreward pays by contest and place, into your [Vault](/help/vault/overview):

| Contest | Winner | Runner-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Daily | 500 IxCredits | 150 IxCredits |
| Weekly | 1,500 IxCredits | 400 IxCredits |
| Monthly | 4,000 IxCredits | 1,000 IxCredits |

Each place pays once. These amounts apply to results dated 15 October 2026 or later; earlier results keep what they paid, and yearly winners don't pay. Runner-up places are only paid where the results record them, which today is the daily contest. Admins can change the amounts.

You're only paid if IxStats can match the winner to your account through a **verified** IxWiki link. Link it in [Settings → IxnayID & Passport → Linked Accounts](/help/getting-started/ixnayid#link-your-wiki-accounts). Results from before the payout date are never back-paid, so linking later does not pay history.

## Trophy cards

Every recorded place, winner or runner-up, in the daily, weekly and monthly contests, has a one-of-one **trophy card** of the winning page. It is a lore card you can keep, trade or list like any other. Monthly winners are Legendary, weekly winners and monthly runner-ups are Epic, and the other places are Rare. Cards exist for results back to 2017. If your wiki account isn't linked yet, the card waits and is given to you once you verify the link. Trophy cards pay nothing and don't count toward your deck value for the daily Vault dividend or the deck-value achievements.

## Where your Lorewards show

- **The Vault.** Open [Achievements](/vault/achievements) and choose the **Lorewards** tab for your wins, runner-up places, streak, what Lorewards have paid you, your place in today's race and your trophy cards.
- **WikiOS.** The [Lorewards page](/util/lorewards) shows today's race (the top ten editors so far today, UTC), the all-time standings and the recent winners. It links to your trophies in the Vault.
- **Your passport.** The front of your [passport](/help/getting-started/ixnayid) has a **Lorewards** stat with your rank and score. Select it to open the same Lorewards view for you: daily, weekly and monthly laurels, runner-up places, your current streak and your trophy cards.
- **The passport showcase.** The Overview tab's **Lorewards standing** panel shows your rank, score, laurels and best streak.
- **Winning articles.** An article that won a Loreward shows a **Loreward winner** badge in its header in WikiOS. Select it to open the winner's trophies, and the trophy card once it exists.

## Score, rank, laurels and streaks

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Score | Your medal score from the wiki's `IxWiki:OOL` page |
| Rank | Your place among all Loreward holders by score |
| Laurels | Your daily, weekly and monthly wins added together |
| Streak | Days in a row with a daily win. Your current streak resets if you go more than a day without one |

## Privacy

Your Lorewards are public by default. To hide them, open your passport, choose **Edit passport** and switch off **Civic accolades**. That hides your Lorewards stat, standing, laurels history and the Lorewards entries in your activity, from everyone. Your trophy cards are listed on a public Lorewards view only while your passport shows your Vault cards.

## Lore Medals

The [Achievements](/vault/achievements) page also lists four **Lore Medals** (the "Order of the Lore") worked out from your Lorewards:

| Medal | How you earn one |
| --- | --- |
| Bronze | A daily win, or a single wiki edit that adds 50,000 bytes or more |
| Silver | A weekly win (a monthly win gives three), or every 10 Bronze |
| Gold | Every 5 Silver |
| Burg Cross | A yearly win, or every 5 Gold |

Lore Medals don't pay IxCredits, don't count toward your achievement points and aren't ribbons. Separate **Lorewards achievements** do: a first Loreward, 5, 25 and 100 daily wins, a first weekly and a first monthly win, a 7-day daily win streak and a first runner-up place.

## Good to know

- Your passport looks up Lorewards by your wiki username, so link your IxWiki account to make sure the right record shows.
- The full results and rankings are also on the wiki's `IxWiki:OOL` pages.
