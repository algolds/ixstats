## What Lorewards are

Lorewards are the community's awards for writing on IxWiki. Every day the community's Lorewards bot picks a **winner** and a **runner-up** from that day's wiki edits, and there are also weekly, monthly and yearly winners. Results are published on the wiki's `IxWiki:OOL` pages. IxStats copies the approved results so your laurels, score and streak show across the platform. Nothing here needs to be claimed: you write on the wiki, and the results come to you.

## Winning a Loreward

1. Write on IxWiki, in [WikiOS](/help/wiki/wikios) or on the classic wiki.
2. The Lorewards bot reviews the day's edits and announces the winner and runner-up. Only approved results count.
3. IxStats copies new results on a schedule, so a new win can take up to a day to show.

## What a win pays

Each winning Loreward (daily, weekly, monthly or yearly) pays **2,500 IxCredits** by default, once per win, into your [Vault](/help/vault/overview). Runner-up places don't pay.

You're only paid if IxStats can match the winner to your account through a **verified** IxWiki link. Link it in [Settings → IxnayID & Passport → Linked Accounts](/help/getting-started/ixnayid#link-your-wiki-accounts). Wins from before you linked are paid on a later sync once you verify.

## Where your Lorewards show

- **Your passport.** The front of your [passport](/help/getting-started/ixnayid) has a **Lorewards** stat with your rank and score. Select it to open your Lorewards accolades: daily, weekly and monthly laurels, runner-up places, your current streak and score, a month-by-month calendar of daily wins and runner-up places, and your laurels history with links to the winning pages.
- **The passport showcase.** The Overview tab's **Lorewards standing** panel shows your rank, score, laurels and best streak.
- **Winning articles.** An article that won a Loreward shows a **Loreward winner** badge in its header in WikiOS. Select it to see the article's awards.

## Score, rank, laurels and streaks

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Score | Your medal score from the wiki's `IxWiki:OOL` page |
| Rank | Your place among all Loreward holders by score |
| Laurels | Your daily, weekly and monthly wins added together |
| Streak | Days in a row with a daily win. Your current streak resets if you go more than a day without one |

## Privacy

Your Lorewards are public by default. To hide them, open your passport, choose **Edit passport** and switch off **Civic accolades**. That hides your Lorewards stat, standing, laurels history and the Lorewards entries in your activity, from everyone.

## Lore Medals

The [Achievements](/achievements) page also lists four **Lore Medals** (the "Order of the Lore") worked out from your Lorewards:

| Medal | How you earn one |
| --- | --- |
| Bronze | A daily win, or a single wiki edit that adds 50,000 bytes or more |
| Silver | A weekly win (a monthly win gives three), or every 10 Bronze |
| Gold | Every 5 Silver |
| Burg Cross | A yearly win, or every 5 Gold |

Lore Medals don't pay IxCredits, don't count toward your achievement points and aren't ribbons.

## Good to know

- Your passport looks up Lorewards by your wiki username, so link your IxWiki account to make sure the right record shows.
- There is no Lorewards leaderboard page in IxStats. The full results and rankings are on the wiki's `IxWiki:OOL` pages.
