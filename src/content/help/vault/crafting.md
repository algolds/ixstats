## What crafting does

Crafting turns cards you own into one rarer card. You pick a recipe, put cards from your collection into its slots, pay its IxCredit cost and craft. Each recipe has a success chance: if it succeeds you get the new card; if it fails you get nothing back. Either way the cards you put in and the IxCredits are used up. Open it from **Vault → Crafting** ([/vault/crafting](/vault/crafting)).

## Kinds of recipe

- **Fusion** combines several cards (for example two Commons) into one card of a higher rarity.
- **Evolution** turns one card into one card of the next rarity up.

The recipes and their numbers are set by the admins. In the starting set, Evolutions take one card and Fusions take two or three; costs run from 200 to 10,000 IxCredits, and the rarer the result, the lower the success chance (from 100% for Common Evolution down to 15% for Mythic Fusion).

## Unlocking recipes

Recipes unlock with your [Vault level](/help/vault/overview#vault-level). The starting recipes need levels 1 to 20. Every recipe is listed, but you can only craft one you've unlocked.

## How to craft

1. Open [Vault → Crafting](/vault/crafting) and choose a recipe from **Select crafting recipe**.
2. Read the recipe card: its type (Fusion or Evolution), **Success rate**, **Cost** in IxCredits and **XP reward**.
3. Select each empty slot (**Click to add card**) and choose a card from your collection. Each card can fill one slot.
4. When every slot is filled and you have enough IxCredits, choose **Craft Card**.
5. Watch the result. **Crafting succeeded** adds the new card to your collection; **Crafting failed** means the materials were consumed and you get no card.

## Which cards you can use

- The cards have to match the recipe: the right rarity, and for some recipes the right card type. If they don't, the craft is refused before anything is spent; the message says what the recipe needs.
- Cards you've listed at auction or offered in a trade are locked and don't appear in the picker.
- A recipe uses exactly the cards it asks for. Extra cards are refused rather than consumed.

## The result

- A successful craft gives you a new card of the recipe's rarity, made by crafting. Crafted cards never drop from [card packs](/help/vault/card-packs).
- The new card starts with the recipe's XP reward.
- Every craft, successful or not, is recorded in your crafting history and your IxCredit transactions.

## Good to know

- **Avoid Mythic Fusion for now.** Mythic isn't a card rarity yet, so the recipe can't make a card. A successful roll gives an error, and a failed roll still uses up your three Legendary cards.
- Admins can switch crafting off for everyone, for example during Vault maintenance. Crafting is then refused before anything is spent.
- Think of crafting as a gamble with your spare duplicates: a failed Fusion loses every card you put in.
