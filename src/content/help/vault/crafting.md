Open a card you own to break a copy down, fuse copies or sign one, and open a card you're missing to craft it. In [Cards → Mine](/vault/cards/mine), **Select** lets you break down, fuse or list several copies at once, and **Dust** shows your dust in each realm. The amounts on this page are the defaults; admins can tune them, and the sheet always shows the current ones.

## Finishes

Every copy has a finish: **Standard**, **Foil** or **Holo**, and **Serialised** for trophies and limited runs. A new copy from a pack, the daily card, a NationStates import or an achievement rolls its finish: about 1 in 12 is Foil and 1 in 50 is Holo.

## Fusing duplicates

Three copies of one card at the same finish fuse into one copy at the next finish: Standard into Foil, Foil into Holo. You keep the copy with the lowest serial number, with its level and XP, and the other two are used up. Signed, Serialised and bound copies can't be fused, and nor can a copy that is listed, at auction or in a trade.

## Breaking down for dust

Breaking a copy down gives **dust of its card's realm**: Ixnay cards give Ixnay dust, NationStates cards give NationStates dust, and so on. Dust stays on your account, kept per realm, and can't be traded.

| Rarity     | Dust from a Standard copy |
| ---------- | ------------------------- |
| Common     | 5                         |
| Uncommon   | 15                        |
| Rare       | 50                        |
| Ultra Rare | 150                       |
| Epic       | 500                       |
| Legendary  | 1,500                     |

A Foil copy gives 3 times as much and a Holo 9 times, so fusing first never changes the dust you get. A copy you imported from NationStates gives a quarter, and so does a copy fused from one. Signed, Serialised and bound copies can't be broken down, and nor can a copy that is listed, at auction or in a trade.

## Crafting

You can craft a card you're missing, up to Epic, from its realm's dust plus IxC: 8 times the dust a Standard copy gives, plus the rarity's floor value in IxC. A Common costs 40 dust and 10 IxC; an Epic costs 4,000 dust and 1,000 IxC. Open the card's sheet and choose **Craft**. A crafted copy is Standard. Legendary, Special, trophy, bound and retired cards can't be crafted, and crafting needs room in your collection.

## Levels

A copy earns XP when it comes from a pack or the daily card, when you trade for it, buy it or win it at auction, and a little for every day you hold it. A copy can gain at most 100 XP a day. Every 1,000 XP is a level, from 1 to 10, and each level above 1 adds 2% to the copy's value, up to 20%.

## Signing a copy

You can sign a copy you own once, with up to 60 characters. The signature stays with the copy wherever it goes, under the name you had when you signed it, and shows on the card's back. You need an IxnayID handle or a nation to sign. If a signature breaks the rules, you can report it from a listing on the card's sheet or from a trade, and staff can clear it.
