## Where packs are

Packs are sold in the Store: [Vault → Store → Packs](/vault/store?view=packs). The Store has four tabs: **My packs** (packs you own but haven't opened), **Packs** (packs for sale), **Cosmetics** and **Account upgrades**. Packs are bought with [IxCredits](/help/vault/ixcredits).

## How to buy and open a pack

1. Open the Store's **Packs** tab. Each pack shows its price; choose its **Pack details and odds** button to see its card count and the chance of each rarity.
2. Buy it. The IxCredits are taken straight away and the pack is yours. (If you don't have enough IxCredits, you can't buy it.)
3. Open it straight away, or later from **My packs**: choose **Open pack** and the pack tears open.
4. The cards fan out face down. Tap a card, swipe, or press the Right or Down arrow key to turn the next one; rarer cards stay in the spotlight a little longer, and from Rare up a card plays the reveal sound (with sound on). **Reveal all** or the Escape key turns the rest, and **Skip** goes straight to the summary.
5. The summary lists what you pulled, and its Rare and rarer cards lean toward your pointer and shimmer (not with Reduce Motion on). Every card is already in your collection: choose a card to open it, **Go to Mine** to see your cards, or open your next pack.

## Odds

Every card in a pack rolls its rarity separately against that pack's odds, which you can see in the pack's details before you buy. As a guide, the default odds are Common 65%, Uncommon 25%, Rare 7%, Ultra Rare 2%, Epic 0.9% and Legendary 0.1%; each pack sets its own, and pricier packs have better odds. If a pack guarantees a minimum rarity and none of its cards reach it, the last card is raised to that rarity.

A pack always gives the odds it shows. If it has no cards left of a rarity it shows or guarantees, it isn't sold, and a pack you already own stays unopened until it has them again. Opening a pack needs room in your collection for all its cards.

## Finishes

Each card you pull rolls a finish: Standard, Foil (about 1 in 12) or Holo (about 1 in 50). See [Dust, fusion and crafting](/help/vault/crafting#finishes).

## Kinds of packs

The seeded line-up (admins can change it) includes:

- **Season packs** drawing from one NationStates card season: Recruit (100–200 IxC, 5 cards), Veteran (500–1,000 IxC) and Commander Elite (2,000–4,000 IxC, 8–10 cards).
- **Cross-pool packs** such as Omni Starter (150), Championship Event (2,500), World Summit (3,500), High Roller Mega (5,000), Lore Master Elite (6,000, lore cards only), Anniversary (8,000), a limited Collector's Edition (12,000) and Founder (15,000).

## Other ways to get cards

- The [daily reward](/help/vault/ixcredits#the-daily-reward) card option. The daily card can come from any realm, at its own odds (by default the same as a pack's). It never gives Special, trophy or nation cards, and it needs room in your collection; if yours is full, take the IxCredits instead.
- [Listings, auctions and trades](/help/vault/trading).
- [Crafting](/help/vault/crafting#crafting) the cards you're missing.
- [Importing your NationStates deck](/help/vault/ns-import).
- Some [achievements](/help/gameplay/achievements) award cards or packs.
