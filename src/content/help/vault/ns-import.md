## What importing does

If you collect NationStates trading cards, you can copy your deck into your IxStats Vault. You prove you own the NationStates nation, and the cards in its deck are added to your collection as NationStates cards. Nothing on NationStates changes, and IxStats never asks for your NationStates password. Start at [Vault → Import](/vault/import).

## How to import

1. **Enter nation.** Type your NationStates nation name.
2. **Visit NS link.** Open the NationStates verification page we link to, while logged in to that nation on NationStates.
3. **Paste code.** Copy the code NationStates shows you and paste it back here, then choose **Verify ownership**.
4. **Import.** Your deck's cards are added to your Vault.

## The import bonus

You earn 50 IxCredits for each newly imported card, up to 5,000 IxCredits. The bonus is paid once per NationStates nation, whichever account imports it first.

## After importing

- Imported cards appear in your **Inventory** and can be put in collections, auctioned or traded like any other card.
- Your NationStates cards are also listed in [Settings](/settings) → **NationStates Card Sync**, and in the NationStates library under Vault → Cards.
- Card artwork comes from NationStates. Each NationStates card shows an attribution notice.

## For NationStates nation owners

If a card of your NationStates nation appears in IxStats and you'd like it removed, open the card's details and use the takedown option at the bottom. You verify you own the nation the same way as above, and the card is retired.
