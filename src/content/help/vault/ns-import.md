## What importing does

If you collect NationStates trading cards, you can copy your deck into your IxStats Vault. You prove you own the NationStates nation, and the cards in its deck are added to your collection as NationStates cards. Nothing on NationStates changes, and IxStats never asks for your NationStates password. Start at [Vault → Import](/vault/import).

## How to import

1. **Enter nation.** Type your NationStates nation name.
2. **Visit NS link.** Open the NationStates verification page we link to, while logged in to that nation on NationStates.
3. **Paste code.** Copy the code NationStates shows you and paste it back here, then choose **Verify ownership**.
4. **Import.** Tick the box to confirm you own or run the nation and allow IxStats to show its flag and cards, then choose **Import deck**. Your deck's cards are added to your Vault in one go: if anything fails, nothing is added and you can try again.

## The import bonus

You earn 50 IxCredits for each newly imported card, up to 5,000 IxCredits. The bonus is paid once per NationStates nation, whichever account imports it first. Each NationStates card is imported once per nation, so importing a deck again adds only the cards that nation had not imported before.

## After importing

- Imported cards appear in [Cards → Mine](/vault/cards/mine) and can be put in showcases, auctioned or traded like any other card.
- Every NationStates card, imported or not, is on the NationStates shelf in [Cards → Explore](/vault/cards/explore?realm=nationstates).
- [Settings](/settings) → **NationStates cards** shows how many NationStates cards you have and the newest, with links to import more and to the takedown option.
- Card artwork comes from NationStates. Each NationStates card shows an attribution notice.

## For NationStates nation owners

If a card of your NationStates nation appears in IxStats and you'd like it removed, open the card and choose **Verify & request takedown** in its **About** section (see [Takedowns](/help/vault/lore-cards#takedowns)). You verify you own the nation the same way as above, and the card's art is hidden at once. You can also ask us at [admin@ixwiki.com](mailto:admin@ixwiki.com) to take down any card's art; we answer within 14 days.
