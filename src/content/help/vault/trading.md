## Where it happens

Everything lives in [Cards → Market](/vault/cards/market), which has four tabs: **Browse** (every live listing and auction), **Trades** (offers with other players), **Wishlist** (the cards you want) and **Selling** (your listings, your auctions, and every running auction you've bid on, marked **Winning** or **Outbid**). A copy you list, auction or offer in a trade is locked until that ends, so it can't be sold or traded twice. Bound cards (trophies and achievement cards) can't be listed, auctioned or traded.

The amounts on this page are the defaults; admins can tune them, and the forms always show the current ones.

## Fixed-price listings

1. Open a card you own and choose **List**.
2. Set a price in whole IxC. You can also feature the listing, which puts it at the top of Browse.
3. Listing costs 5 IxC, or 10 IxC featured. A listing runs for 14 days.

If you cancel it in **Selling**, or it expires, the copy comes back to you and half the listing fee is refunded. When it sells, you get the price minus the market fee and a "Your listing sold" notice.

## Selling at auction

1. Open a card you own and choose **Sell at auction**, or open **Selling** and choose **Sell a card**. Pick the copy (search for it, or page through your cards).
2. Set a **starting price** and, if you like, a **buy-now price** (higher than the starting price).
3. Choose how long it runs: **24 hours**, **3 days** or **7 days**.
4. Pay the listing fee, the same as for a fixed-price listing.

If nobody bids, the card comes back to you and half the listing fee is refunded. You also get half back if you cancel an auction before anyone bids.

## Buying

- **Browse** shows listings and auctions together, featured ones first. Filter by realm and rarity, and sort by ending soon, newest or price. A card's sheet also links to its own listings, and you can buy a listing from the sheet.
- **Buy** a listing at its price.
- **Bid** at least 5% more than the current highest bid (or the starting price, before anyone has bid). Your IxC are held while you lead; if you're outbid they come straight back, you're told, and the auction shows as **Outbid** in **Selling**, with **Raise bid**.
- **Buy now** at the buy-now price to end an auction at once.
- A bid in the last 5 minutes adds 1 minute, so there's always time to answer.

When an auction ends, the highest bidder gets the card and the seller gets the price minus the market fee, and everyone who bid is told. Ended auctions and expired listings are settled by a regular background job, so there can be a short wait before the card and credits move.

## Direct trades

1. Open **Trades** and choose **New trade**, then find the other player by their IxnayID handle. Or open a card you own and choose **Offer in a trade**. Before you build anything, you see whether they take trade offers from you.
2. Fill the two columns, **You give** and **You get**, with cards and IxC from each collection. A trade needs at least one card, so you can offer IxC for a card. If one side gives nothing, the trade is a gift.
3. Cards on your wishlist are marked among theirs, and cards on their wishlist are marked among yours.
4. Send it, with a note if you like. The cards you give are held while the offer is open.

They can **accept**, **decline**, or **counter**: a counter edits the same table and sends it back, and then their cards are the ones held. An offer lasts 24 hours, and you can withdraw one you sent. **Trades** lists the offers waiting for you (**Incoming**), the ones you sent (**Sent**) and your **History** of trades and auctions. Signed copies can be traded.

## Fees and royalties

- The market fee is 10% of a sale over 100 IxC. The same fee applies to IxC that change hands in a trade over 100 IxC, and the trade shows it before you send. A trade of cards for cards pays no fee.
- When a nation card sells, or is paid for with IxC in a trade, 2% of the amount goes to that nation's owner. It comes out of the market fee, so a deal too small to pay a fee pays no royalty. Nothing is paid when the nation's owner is the buyer or the seller, or when the nation has no owner.

## Wishlist

Add a card to your wishlist from its sheet. **Wishlist** lists your cards with any listings and auctions running for them. Players building a trade with you see your wishlist; everyone else sees only how many players want a card.

## Notices about a card

Open a card's sheet to mute it: you get no notices about that card, such as being outbid on it or a listing of it selling.
