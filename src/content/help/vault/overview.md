## What the Vault is

The [Vault](/vault) holds your IxCredits (the platform currency), your trading cards and your progress. Everyone with an account has one; you don't need a nation to use it, though a nation earns you a daily dividend. Open it from **Vault** in the top menu. Its sidebar has **Dashboard**, **Daily reward**, **Cards**, **Collections**, **Marketplace**, **Crafting** and **Import**.

## Dashboard

- Your **IxCredits** balance, **Vault level** and progress to the next level, and your login **streak**.
- **Yield projections:** your expected daily, weekly and monthly dividend from your nation, with the budget multiplier and tier bonus behind it, and how much of today's active and social earning caps you've used.
- Your collection's value, card holdings, milestones, recent activity and a showcase of your best cards.

## The daily reward

Once per UTC day you can claim a daily reward. You choose **either**:

- **IxCredits:** a random roll that grows with your Vault level and your streak. It's paid up to your remaining daily earning cap (100 IxC for active earnings by default), so on most days the payout is capped.
- **A card:** one random card added to your collection.

The claim prompt opens by itself at most once a day. If you close it, claim later from the Vault widget (in the Vault and Dashboard sidebars). Claiming on consecutive UTC days builds your streak; missing a day resets it to 1. See [IxCredits](/help/vault/ixcredits).

## Cards

**Cards** has three tabs:

- **Card Gallery:** browse every card in the game, including NationStates and lore cards.
- **Inventory:** the cards you own. Filter by rarity, type, season, level and value, and switch between grid, list and compact views.
- **Collections:** group your cards into named collections. Public collections can be browsed at [/explore/collections](/explore/collections).

Select a card to see it up close with its details.

## Card types and rarities

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Nation | A country card |
| Lore | Made from a wiki article. See [Lore Cards](/help/vault/lore-cards) |
| NationStates | Imported from NationStates. See [Importing NationStates Cards](/help/vault/ns-import) |
| Special | Commemorative and event cards |
| Community | Contest and community cards |

Rarities, from most to least common: **Common**, **Uncommon**, **Rare**, **Ultra Rare**, **Epic** and **Legendary**. Rarer cards have stronger visual effects.

## Marketplace

**Marketplace** has three tabs: **Vault Shop** (card packs, plus cosmetics and account upgrades; see [Card Packs](/help/vault/card-packs) and [Vault Shop Items](/help/vault/shop-items)), **Auctions** and **Trading** (see [Auctions & Trading](/help/vault/trading)).

## Vault level

You gain 1 XP for every IxCredit you earn, and each 1,000 XP is a level. A higher level makes the daily IxCredit roll larger and unlocks more [crafting](/help/vault/crafting) recipes.

## Recycling cards

You can recycle ("junk") a card you don't want for a small amount of IxCredits based on its rarity. Cards listed at auction or offered in a trade are locked and can't be recycled.
