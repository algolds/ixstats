## What the Vault is

The [Vault](/vault) holds your IxCredits (the platform currency), your trading cards, your achievements and your progress. Everyone with an account has one; you don't need a nation to use it, and a nation plays no part in it. Open it from **Vault** in the app menu. Its sidebar has five rows: **Wallet**, **Daily reward**, **Achievements**, **Cards** and **Store**. The wallet card at the foot of the sidebar shows your balance and today's earnings, and opens to show what you earned today by source and your achievement progress.

## Wallet

The Wallet is your account statement.

- At the top: your **IxCredits** balance, **Vault level**, what you earned today, your net worth (credits plus the value of your cards) and your login **streak**, with a button to claim the daily reward or open waiting packs when they apply.
- The **statement** lists every change to your balance, newest first and grouped by day, with the balance after each one. Filter it to **Earned** or **Spent**, and choose **Show earlier** for older rows. A **Vault dividend** row expands to show how that day's amount was worked out; Loreward rows name the contest and place.
- Beside it, your **holdings**: achievements unlocked, Lorewards, cards and packs waiting, and a featured item from the Store.

## The daily reward

Once per UTC day you can claim a daily reward. You choose **either**:

- **IxCredits:** a random roll that grows with your Vault level and your streak. It's paid up to your remaining daily earning cap (100 IxC for active earnings by default), so on most days the payout is capped.
- **A card:** one random card added to your collection.

The claim prompt opens by itself at most once a day. If you close it, claim later from the **Daily reward** row or the wallet card in the sidebar. Claiming on consecutive UTC days builds your streak; missing a day resets it to 1. See [IxCredits](/help/vault/ixcredits).

## Cards

**Cards** has four tabs:

- **[Mine](/vault/cards/mine):** the cards you own. Filter by realm, rarity, finish, type, category and duplicates, search, sort, and switch between a grid and a list with each card's value. Choose **Select** to pick several cards and add them to a showcase.
- **[Sets](/vault/cards/sets):** your showcases, sets of your cards you put together and name. A showcase can be public or private, and you can pin one public showcase: it is what your wallet and your passport show. Without a pinned showcase they show your most valuable cards.
- **[Market](/vault/cards/market):** auctions and trades. See [Auctions & Trading](/help/vault/trading).
- **[Explore](/vault/cards/explore):** every realm's cards, realm by realm, then a grid you can filter by any realm with cards. Trophy cards aren't in Explore: yours are on the Lorewards tab of [Achievements](/vault/achievements?tab=lorewards), and a player's are on their passport. A card you haven't collected shows as a silhouette (its title, realm, rarity and number, but not its art). You can also suggest a new lore card here; see [Lore Cards](/help/vault/lore-cards).

Select a card to open its sheet: the card itself (turn it over with Space or F to see its back), your copies of it, its market, and what it is about. **Copy link** copies a link that opens the same card. Without signing in, anyone can open the cards a passport or a public showcase shows, and cards on a live auction; any other card asks you to sign in. Importing a NationStates deck is also part of Cards; see [Importing NationStates Cards](/help/vault/ns-import).

## Card types and rarities

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Nation | A country card |
| Lore | Made from a wiki article. See [Lore Cards](/help/vault/lore-cards) |
| NationStates | Imported from NationStates. See [Importing NationStates Cards](/help/vault/ns-import) |
| Special | Commemorative, event and achievement cards |
| Trophy | Loreward and sports trophy cards. See [Lorewards](/help/wiki/lorewards#trophy-cards) |

Bound cards (trophies and achievement cards) stay with the account that earned them: they can't be traded or auctioned.

Rarities, from most to least common: **Common**, **Uncommon**, **Rare**, **Ultra Rare**, **Epic** and **Legendary**. A card's rarity is the colour of its edge and its gem. A copy of a card also has a finish (Standard, Foil, Holo or Serialised), which shows as a sheen.

## Store

The [Store](/vault/store?view=packs) has four tabs: **My packs** (packs you own and haven't opened), **Packs** (packs for sale; see [Card Packs](/help/vault/card-packs)), **Cosmetics** and **Account upgrades** (see [Vault Shop Items](/help/vault/shop-items)). Auctions and trades are in Cards → **Market**.

## Vault level

You gain 1 XP for every IxCredit you earn, and each 1,000 XP is a level. A higher level makes the daily IxCredit roll larger.

## Also in the Vault

- [Achievements](/help/gameplay/achievements) have their own row in the sidebar, with the Lorewards tab and a leaderboard by achievement points. See also the nation [Leaderboards](/help/gameplay/leaderboards).
- The **Exchange** moved to Labs, and IxCredits can no longer be converted to Sovereigns.
- **Crafting** is retired for now. Your cards and IxCredits are untouched.
- **Collections** are now showcases, in Cards → **Sets**: your collections became showcases holding the cards you still own, and likes and comments are gone.
