## What this section is for

The Economy section of MyCountry ([/mycountry/economy](/mycountry/economy)) shows how your economy is doing and holds your fiscal controls: department budgets, tax rates and tariffs, plus your trade partners. It has four tabs: **Report**, **Budget**, **Taxes** and **Trade**. The headline strip at the top shows total GDP, GDP growth, your Economic vitality score and government efficiency.

> [!WARNING]
> **Budgets and tax rates don't change GDP growth today.** Your tax rates set the revenue every tab shows (one figure: your rates on today's GDP), and the budget sets spending. GDP moves with your growth rate, tier, directives, issue outcomes and world events. See [How Your Economy Is Calculated](/help/economy/calculations).

## Report

A read-out of your economy: sector output, labor market (unemployment, youth unemployment, female participation, wages, informal work) and income and wealth distribution (the top 10% wealth share and middle class share are worked out from your income classes). Most of these figures were generated from your choices when you founded your nation; anything not recorded shows "—", never a stand-in number.

## Budget

Your government budget split across departments, and the cost of maintaining infrastructure.

1. Open **Budget**.
2. Adjust how much each department receives.
3. Watch the totals and the fiscal balance to see whether you're spending more than you raise. Revenue here is the same figure as in **Taxes**: your saved rates on today's GDP, or your founding estimate until you save a rate.

Your budget split does not earn IxCredits. Some [directives](/help/mycountry/executive) also nudge department allocations.

## Taxes

Set your national tax rates in **National Tax Rate Controls**:

| Tax | Range |
| --- | --- |
| Income tax | 0–60% |
| Corporate tax | 0–50% |
| VAT and sales tax | 0–30% |
| Import tariff | 0–25% |
| Wealth tax | 0–10% |
| Capital gains tax | 0–40% |

Each rate saves when you let go of its slider; a tax you haven't set yet shows **Not set**. **Tax Revenue Projections** shows what each tax brings in, and the insights panel marks where your overall tax burden sits against an "optimal zone" of 15–35%. More in [Taxes & Revenue](/help/economy/tax-system).

## Trade

See your exports, imports and trade balance, try out sector tariffs in a planner (not saved), and see which partners you have a trade treaty with. More in [Trade & Commerce](/help/economy/trade).

## Tips

- Balance the budget before chasing growth: large deficits show up in your debt-to-GDP ranking in the [World Census](/help/mycountry/overview#world-census).
- To grow GDP, use economy or infrastructure [directives](/help/mycountry/executive) and answer [issues](/help/gameplay/national-issues) with growth in mind.
- Try ideas first in the [modeling sandbox](/help/economy/modeling).
