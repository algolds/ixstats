## What this section is for

The Economy section of MyCountry ([/mycountry/economy](/mycountry/economy)) shows how your economy is doing and holds your fiscal controls: department budgets, tax rates, tariffs and trade partners. It has four tabs: **Economic Report**, **National Budget**, **Fiscal Policy** and **Trade & Commerce**. The headline strip at the top shows total GDP, GDP growth, your Economic vitality score and government efficiency.

> [!WARNING]
> **Budgets and tax rates don't change GDP growth today.** They change your revenue and spending figures and your daily IxCredit dividend. GDP moves with your growth rate, tier, directives, issue outcomes and world events. See [How Your Economy Is Calculated](/help/economy/calculations).

## Economic Report

A read-out of your economy: sector output, labor market (unemployment, youth unemployment, wages, informal work) and income and wealth distribution. Most of these figures come from what you entered in the builder; change them in the [Country Editor](/help/mycountry/editor) (Advanced mode, Economics step).

## National Budget

Your government budget split across departments, and the cost of maintaining infrastructure.

1. Open **National Budget**.
2. Adjust how much each department receives.
3. Watch the totals and the revenue banner to see whether you're spending more than you raise.

How you split the budget feeds into your daily IxCredit dividend through a budget multiplier: each department's sub-budgets carry a multiplier, weighted by that department's share of the budget (1× when you have no departments set up). Some [directives](/help/mycountry/executive) also nudge department allocations.

## Fiscal Policy

Set your national tax rates in **National Tax Rate Controls**:

| Tax | Range |
| --- | --- |
| Income tax | 0–60% |
| Corporate tax | 0–50% |
| VAT / sales tax | 0–30% |
| Tariff rate | 0–25% |
| Wealth tax | 0–10% |
| Capital gains tax | 0–40% |

Changes save automatically. **Tax Revenue Projections** shows what each tax brings in, and the insights panel marks where your overall tax burden sits against an "optimal zone" of 15–35%. More in [Taxes & Revenue](/help/economy/tax-system).

## Trade & Commerce

Set tariffs by sector, manage your trade partners, and see a summary of your trade position. More in [Trade & Commerce](/help/economy/trade).

## Tips

- Balance the budget before chasing growth: large deficits show up in your debt-to-GDP ranking in the [World Census](/help/mycountry/overview#world-census).
- To grow GDP, use economy or infrastructure [directives](/help/mycountry/executive) and answer [issues](/help/gameplay/national-issues) with growth in mind.
- Try ideas first in the [modeling sandbox](/help/economy/modeling).
