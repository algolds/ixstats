## What the Canvas editor is

The Canvas editor is WikiOS's visual editor: you write and format text the way you would in a document, instead of typing wikitext. You'll meet it in two places:

- **Wiki articles.** When you edit or create an article in the [wiki](/help/wiki/wikios), you can choose the **Canvas editor** or the **Source editor**.
- **Your nation's dossier.** On a country's profile, the **Dossier** tab has a **Native canvas lore** view where you can write lore documents in the same editor.

## Editing a wiki article in Canvas

1. Open an article and choose edit (or create a page; the wizard asks which editor you want, and **Remember choice** keeps it for next time).
2. Use the **Source / Canvas** switch at the top of the editor to change modes at any time.
3. In Canvas you can work with text blocks, tables and infoboxes, insert things from the slash menu, and fill in templates through forms.

Your work in progress is kept as a draft in this browser, so a reload doesn't lose it. Saving publishes the edit to the wiki, with the same rules as any wiki edit; see [The Wiki (WikiOS)](/help/wiki/wikios).

## Dossier lore documents

Open a country's profile, go to **Dossier**, and switch to **Native canvas lore**.

- **New document** opens the Canvas editor in a window. Give it a title, write the lore and choose a clearance (**Public**, **Alliance** or **Private**).
- To keep your text, first use the editor's own save menu (the save icon) and choose **Save session**, confirm in the panel that opens, then choose **Save document**. The editor's save only passes your text to the document window; nothing is published to the wiki. If you choose **Save document** without it, the document keeps its earlier text.
- **Import Files** turns `.md`, `.txt` or `.json` files into documents. Headings (`#` to `###`) in a Markdown or text file split it into separate documents, and you can set each one's clearance before importing.
- Edit or delete a document from its card.

> [!WARNING]
> Dossier lore documents are saved **in this browser only**. Other players can't see them, they don't appear on other devices, and clearing your browser data deletes them. The clearance setting is a label for now; it doesn't control who can see a document. To share lore with other players, put it in a [wiki](/help/wiki/wikios) article.

The other Dossier view, the wiki sections, comes from your nation's wiki article. If your nation has no article yet, the dossier offers **Create page on WikiOS**.
