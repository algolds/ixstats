## What realms are

A realm is a world that nations live in, a bit like a NationStates region. **IxWorld** is the main realm; other realms are run by their founders. Each nation belongs to one realm, and rankings in the [World Census](/help/mycountry/overview#world-census) and the [leaderboards](/help/gameplay/leaderboards) are per realm. All realms share the same [IxTime](/help/getting-started/ixtime) clock.

## Finding realms

The [realm directory](/realms) lists every public, active realm (IxWorld always), with how many nations it has, how many are unclaimed, how many you own, the realm's nation limit, and recent board activity. Unlisted realms can still be opened by link.

Each realm has a page at `/r/<realm>` with its nations and a claim list, and a **board** at `/r/<realm>/board`. The **Community** panel links to the realm's forum, Discord, wiki or map, and the header shows the realm's **in-world date** if its founder set one.

## Claiming a nation

1. Open the realm's page from the [directory](/realms) (or use **Link to Existing Country** in [Setup](/setup) for IxWorld).
2. In the claim list, choose an unclaimed nation and submit your claim. If the realm has **rules** (its **Rules** tab), read them and tick **I have read the realm's rules** first; you can't claim without it.
3. A moderator (a site admin or the realm's founder) reviews it. If you've [verified the wiki account](/help/getting-started/ixnayid#link-your-wiki-accounts) that created that nation's wiki page, the claim is approved immediately.

The directory shows **Join · claim a nation** (or **Claim another nation**) while you're under the realm's limit.

## Nation limits

How many nations you can hold in a realm is the lower of:

- the **realm's limit** (set by its admins, 1 by default, up to 20), and
- your **account limit**: 1 for free accounts, 5 for [Premium](/help/getting-started/premium).

The builder and claims both check this.

## Playing more than one nation

If you own several nations, choose which one you're playing as with the nation switcher in the user menu, or **Play as** on a realm page. MyCountry, directives and proposals act as that nation. Your daily IxCredit dividend always comes from your first (earliest-created) nation, whichever one you're playing as.

## Realm boards

A realm board is a shared space for the realm's nations, with three tabs:

- **Board:** a feed. Anyone can read it; owners of a nation in the realm can post (as a persona of one of their nations there).
- **Chat:** a group conversation for members.
- **Realm feed:** ThinkPages posts from the realm's nations.

You join a board automatically on your first visit if you own a nation in the realm. Site admins and the realm's founder can remove posts.

## Good to know

- Founding a new realm isn't open to players yet; realms are created by admins.
- A realm can show its own in-world date (set by its founder), but it still runs on the shared IxTime clock.
- Founders and officers with the appearance power set the realm's links, rules and in-world date in **Manage**.
