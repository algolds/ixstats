## What Explore is

[Explore](/explore) is a searchable, sortable list of every nation in your realm. It sits in the sidebar under **Realms**, next to **Countries**, and anyone can use it, signed in or not.

- **Countries** ([/countries](/countries)) shows nations in a shuffled order, which is good for discovering new ones. See [Exploring Countries](/help/world/countries).
- **Explore** ([/explore](/explore)) is for finding a particular kind of nation: filter, sort, page through results and compare.

Both list the nations of your active nation's realm (IxWorld if you're signed out or have no nation). Only the Countries page can show another realm, by adding `?realm=<realm>` to its address.

## Filtering and sorting

On a wide screen the filters sit on the left; on a phone, choose **Show filters**.

- **Search** by name, continent or region.
- **Filter** by economic tier, continent, region (once you've picked a continent) and population range. **Clear all** resets them.
- **Sort** by name, population, GDP per person, total GDP, economic tier, continent, region, land area or population density, up or down.

The header totals the population and GDP of the nations that match. Results show nine to a page. Select a nation to open its profile.

## Comparing nations

Choose **Compare countries** to open the comparison window. Add up to eight nations and see their figures side by side.

## Collections

**Collections** ([/explore/collections](/explore/collections)), also under **Realms** in the sidebar, is something different: a gallery of players' public [Vault](/help/vault/overview) card collections. Search them, sort by newest, most valuable, most cards or top rated, and see the collection leaderboard.

## What you see on a profile

Every figure on a nation's profile comes from live data, and only the nation's public record is shown to visitors: enacted directives, resolved national issues, statistics and lore. Drafts, open issues and detailed budgets stay private to the nation's owner. See [Exploring Countries](/help/world/countries) for the profile and its actions.
