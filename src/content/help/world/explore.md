## What Explore is

The sidebar's **Explore** opens the [realm directory](/realms), where you find, search and join realms. A realm's nations are on its **Nations** tab; **My realm** opens yours. The old `/explore` and `/countries` addresses open it too. See [Exploring Countries](/help/world/countries) for the card grid, its search and filters, and claiming a nation.

## Comparing nations

On a nation's profile, choose **Compare** to open the comparison window. Add up to eight nations and see their figures side by side.

## Collections

**Collections** ([/explore/collections](/explore/collections)) is something different: a gallery of players' public [Vault](/help/vault/overview) card collections. Search them, sort by newest, most valuable, most cards or top rated, and see the collection leaderboard.

## What you see on a profile

Every figure on a nation's profile comes from live data, and only the nation's public record is shown to visitors: enacted directives, resolved national issues, statistics and lore. Drafts, open issues and detailed budgets stay private to the nation's owner. See [Exploring Countries](/help/world/countries) for the profile and its actions.
