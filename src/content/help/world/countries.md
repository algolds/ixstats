## A realm's nations

Every nation belongs to a realm, so a realm's nations live on its **Nations** tab (`/r/<realm>/nations`). **My realm**, under **Realms** in the sidebar, opens your realm's Nations tab: your active nation's realm, or Ixnay if you're signed out or have no nation. Anyone can browse it, signed in or not. The old [/countries](/countries) address opens it too, and `/countries?realm=<realm>` opens another realm's.

- Each nation is a **flag card**. Hover it for its population and GDP; select it to expand its details, or choose **View** to open its profile.
- Your own nations come first; the rest are shuffled once per visit. Choose **Feeling lucky** to open a random nation from the ones shown.
- **Search** by name, economic tier, continent or region, and **filter** by economic tier. The figures above the cards total the nations shown; open the first one to filter by continent, or another to see the top five.
- A card's corner shows the nation's state: **Unclaimed**, **Pending review**, **Active** (your active nation) or **Yours**. Hover the card and it becomes the action: **Claim** an unclaimed nation while the realm takes claims, **Play as** one of yours, or **Leave realm** with your active nation. To leave with another of your nations, play as it first.

To find other realms, search across them or join one, choose **Explore**, which opens the [realm directory](/realms). See [Realms & Claiming a Nation](/help/world/realms).

## A country's profile

Select a nation to open its profile at `/countries/<name>`. The profile reads like a passport: the top shows its map, flag, name, key figures and standing (and, if it has an active owner, the owner's top achievement ribbons), and four tabs sit underneath:

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Factbook** | The nation at a glance: **Economy & people**, **State** and **Foreign affairs**. Choose **More** on any of them for the full figures |
| **Map** | Its territory, land facts, regions and map stories. Choose a region or a story to fly the map there |
| **Lore** | Its wiki article, chapter by chapter, then its lore pieces |
| **History** | Its chronicle and recent activity as one timeline |

Every figure comes from live data. Visitors see the nation's public record only: enacted directives and resolved national issues, not drafts, open issues or detailed budgets.

## Country Actions

Choose **Country actions** on another nation's profile to:

- **Follow Nation** to see its activity in your Following feed.
- **Establish an embassy** there. See [Embassies](/help/diplomacy/embassies).
- **Propose** a free trade agreement or military alliance (sent to their Diplomacy Inbox), or impose a trade embargo or sanctions (immediate). See [Foreign Affairs](/help/mycountry/diplomacy).
- **Request a meeting** or **congratulate** them on an achievement.
- **Copy Profile Link**, or open the nation's wiki article.

On your own nation the button is **Manage**: it turns the profile's top over to your cover choice, your private to-dos, your lore team and a link to edit your country.

## Lore pieces

A nation's lore can go beyond its main wiki article: its people, places, events and organisations, each on its own wiki page. These pages show on the **Lore** tab after the article, grouped as People, Places, Events and Organisations, and anyone can read them there or choose **Open in WikiOS**. They are ordinary wiki pages, so anyone with wiki rights can edit them on the wiki too.

Writing lore from the profile opens with WikiOS editing, which arrives with the WikiOS launch. It is for nations in Ixnay, by the nation's owner and the co-authors they invite:

- **Write lore** on the Lore tab starts a new piece. Choose what it is about, give it a title (the profile checks that no page has it yet), fill in a few facts for its infobox, then write it in the WikiOS editor. Saving publishes the page under your own name.
- On the **Map** tab, choose a region, city or story, then **Write about this place**: the title, the type and the location are filled in for you.
- On the **History** tab, the pencil on an entry is **Write about this event**: the title, the type and the date are filled in.
- **Edit** beside a piece opens it in the same writer.

A piece is listed because its page carries the category `[[Category:<Nation> lore]]`, which the writer adds for you. Keep that line when you edit, or the piece leaves the Lore tab. Any page in that category shows up, even one written straight in WikiOS.

As the owner you can also:

- **Pin** a piece to put it first in its group, and **Unpin** it.
- **Hide** a piece from the Lore tab. The page stays on the wiki.
- **Add an existing page** by its title, to list a page that is not in the category, or to bring back one you hid.
- Choose **Manage**, then **Lore team**, to add co-authors by their IxnayID handle or remove them. A co-author you remove loses the writing buttons the next time they load the page.
