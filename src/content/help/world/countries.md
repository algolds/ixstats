## The country directory

**My realm** ([/countries](/countries)), under **Realms** in the sidebar, lists every nation in your realm (or another realm with `?realm=<realm>` in the address). Anyone can browse it, signed in or not.

- **Search** by name, continent or region.
- **Filter** by economic tier, continent, region and population range, and **sort** by any figure.
- **Compare** up to eight nations side by side. See [Comparing Nations](/help/world/explore).

To find other realms, search across them or join one, choose **Explore**, which opens the [realm directory](/realms). See [Realms & Claiming a Nation](/help/world/realms).

## A country's profile

Select a nation to open its profile at `/countries/<name>`. The header shows its flag, name, key figures and, if it has an active owner, the owner's top achievement ribbons. The profile has four tabs:

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | The nation at a glance: vitals, economy, government, land, relations and its chronicle |
| **Factbook** | The full figures, with pages for Economy, Labor, Government and Geography |
| **Dossier** | Its lore from the wiki, plus lore documents you write in the [Canvas editor](/help/mycountry/canvas-editor), which are kept in your own browser |
| **Activity** | Its recent activity and history |

Every figure comes from live data. Visitors see the nation's public record only: enacted directives and resolved national issues, not drafts, open issues or detailed budgets.

## Country Actions

Open **Country Actions** on another nation's profile to:

- **Follow Nation** to see its activity in your Following feed.
- **Establish an embassy** there. See [Embassies](/help/diplomacy/embassies).
- **Propose** a free trade agreement or military alliance (sent to their Diplomacy Inbox), or impose a trade embargo or sanctions (immediate). See [Foreign Affairs](/help/mycountry/diplomacy).
- **Request a meeting** or **congratulate** them on an achievement.
- **Copy Profile Link**, or open the nation's wiki article.

On your own nation the menu is called **Country Management**.

## Other ways to explore

- [IxMaps](/help/world/maps) to see nations on the globe.
- [Leaderboards](/help/gameplay/leaderboards) to rank them.
- [Economic Modeling](/help/economy/modeling) on any nation, to project its economy.
- The [wiki](/help/wiki/wikios) for each nation's full lore.
