## What IxMaps is

[IxMaps](/maps) is the interactive map of IxWorld. Zoomed out it is a globe; as you zoom in it flattens into a regular map. Every nation has territory on it, and you can open the map for another realm from that realm's page (see [Realms & Claiming a Nation](/help/world/realms)).

## Finding a nation

- **Search** with Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac), pick a result with the arrow keys and Enter.
- **Click a nation** to open its panel: economic data, a wiki introduction, neighbours and sovereignty.
- The layers menu also switches cities, regions, points of interest, story pins and map labels on and off.

## Overlays

Colour overlays shade every nation by one measure, and only one of them is on at a time. Open **Layers** in the top-left toolbar to switch them, and read the legend in the bottom-left corner.

| Overlay | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Wealth Map | GDP per capita |
| Population Density | Sparse to dense |
| Economic Tier | Nations shaded by economic tier |
| Economic Vitality | Overall economic health |
| Health Map | National health |
| Trade Balance | Surplus to deficit |
| Canon Density | How densely a nation is covered by canon stories |
| Crisis Hotspots | An estimated crisis risk from geography, not a list of live crises |
| Diplomatic Network | Friendly, neutral and hostile relationships |
| Transport Routes | Rail, highways, roads and shipping |

## Tools and shortcuts

The map has a **Measure** tool (M) for point-to-point distance and a **Pin** tool for point info. Pan with the arrow keys or WASD, zoom with + and -, press R to reset the view, P to cycle the projection and ? for the full shortcut list. Shortcuts are ignored while you are typing.

## The timeline

When a nation's borders have a recorded history, a **Timeline** pill appears at the bottom right. Open it and drag the scrubber to see the political map at an earlier [IxTime](/help/getting-started/ixtime).

## Editing your own nation

If you own a nation you can edit its territory, cities, regions and routes from the map; the editor opens over the map.
