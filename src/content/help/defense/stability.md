## What stability is

Stability (0 to 100) measures how calm and secure your nation is at home. It's shown on the [MyCountry home page](/help/mycountry/overview) for every player, counts in the World Census, and is broken down in [MyCountry → Defense](/mycountry/defense) → **Internal Stability**.

## How the score is made

The score is a weighted mix of six measures, each worked out from your economy, demographics and politics:

| Measure | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Social cohesion | 25% |
| Trust in government | 20% |
| Low crime | 20% |
| Low ethnic tension | 15% |
| Low riot risk | 10% |
| Policing effectiveness | 10% |

Inequality, unemployment, poverty and political polarisation push the measures down. Some inputs use default values until your nation has data for them.

## What moves it

- **National issues** and **directives.** Many issue responses change stability directly, and defense directives raise it. These changes are kept when the score is recalculated.
- **Elections.** A decisive result raises stability; a narrow one lowers it.
- **Your economy.** Growth, unemployment and inequality feed the underlying measures.

The panel also shows a trend: improving, stable, declining or critical.

## Tips

- Stability is visible to every player, with or without Premium.
- If stability is falling, look at recent issue responses in your change log first; they are the most common cause.
