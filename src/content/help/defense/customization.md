> [!WARNING]
> **Preview feature**
>
> Defense is a MyCountry Premium feature in preview. Every nation can open the **Defense** tile in MyCountry, but without Premium it's a read-only preview — building forces and launching operations need Premium.

## Customization Options

- Adjust strategic module emphasis (cyber, air defense, naval readiness, etc.).
- Assign doctrines and readiness postures that affect compliance tasks and analytics.
- Define bespoke task forces through editor flows for narrative flavour.

## Workflow

> **Steps**
>
> 1. Open the Defense tab and select the module or task force you want to configure.
> 2. Apply your changes — they save right away.
> 3. Check your new numbers in the readiness gauges.

## Documentation

- Reflect changes in ThinkPages posts or policy documents for story continuity.
- Audit updates by checking the notification feed and [Alerts & Notifications](/help/intelligence/alerts) to ensure signals align.
