## What diplomatic events are

Diplomatic events (also called scenarios) are short situations between your nation and another one, such as a border dispute, a trade negotiation, a cultural misunderstanding, an intelligence breach, a humanitarian crisis, alliance pressure, a sanctions debate, a technology transfer request, a diplomatic incident, a mediation opportunity, an embassy security threat or a treaty renewal. Each one describes the situation and gives you several responses. They appear in [MyCountry → Diplomacy](/mycountry/diplomacy) → **Diplomatic Events**.

## Where they come from

- **Admins** write scenarios and assign them to nations through the admin console.
- **Cultural exchanges.** In an exchange's details you can choose **Generate Scenario** to create a situation involving one of the participating nations.

Scenarios have an expiry date; expired ones disappear from the active list.

## How to respond

1. Open **Diplomatic Events** and select an active event.
2. Read the situation and each response. Responses are marked with a risk level (low, medium, high, extreme).
3. Choose a response.

## What a response does

- Your choice and its estimated cultural, diplomatic and economic impact are recorded in your diplomatic history.
- You earn IxCredits: 10 for responding, +5 for a high-stakes event, and +2, +5 or +8 for a medium, high or extreme-risk response (10 to 23 IxC, within the 100 IxC daily active-earning cap).
- Responses don't currently change relationship strength or your economy.

## Tips

- Riskier responses pay a few more IxCredits and make for a more dramatic story; they don't cost you anything else today.
- Write up what happened on the [feed](/help/social/thinkpages) or in the [wiki](/help/wiki/wikios) to make it part of your nation's story.
