## What the Exchange is

The [Exchange](/exchange) is IxStates' market terminal. It shows everything happening in your realm's economy: every trade, tender, dividend and bankruptcy, and every tax or tariff change a nation makes. When you want to act on what you see, a trade takes a tap or two. Anyone can watch, signed in or not. To trade, run a company or act for a nation, sign in.

Money on the Exchange is the **Sovereign** (₷). It is kept apart from IxCredits: you can't convert one into the other.

## The front page

The [front page](/exchange) is your realm at a glance:

- **The realm index**: one line for the whole market, built from the four sector indices and weighted by the realm's economy. Switch the range with **1D**, **1W**, **1M** and **All**.
- **Today in business**: the stories that matter, such as tenders won, dividends paid, bonds issued or defaulted, and policy changes. Each one shows who it comes from and the price move it caused.
- **Sectors**, **Movers** and **Nation bonds**: what moved today, and what nations pay to borrow.
- **Live activity**: every trade and tender win as it happens. Players appear as "a private investor", never by username.

The realm you watch is in the header. It starts on your own nation's realm.

## Markets and trading

[Markets](/exchange/markets) lists everything you can buy:

- **Companies**: player-run companies. Open one to see its price chart, key figures, owners, filings, votes and news.
- **Bonds**: loans to nations. Each pays a weekly coupon from the nation's treasury and repays the full price in its last week.
- **Funds**: sector index funds, the low-effort way to invest. Put ₷ into a fund and sell a share of your units later.

Press **Trade** on any company, bond or fund. Choose **Buy** or **Sell**, enter an amount, check the estimate and confirm. Shares trade at the best price in the order book, and **Advanced: order book** on a company page shows the bids and asks. If a trade fails on a bad connection, confirming again won't charge you twice.

## Watching

Press the star on a company, bond or nation to add it to your watchlist. You get a notification when it makes the news. Your [Portfolio](/exchange/portfolio) shows what you hold and what it is worth, your open orders (which you can cancel), your watchlist, and any shareholder votes waiting for you.

[Leaderboards](/exchange/leaderboards) ranks the realm's biggest companies, richest treasuries and largest portfolios.

## Bonds, ratings and default

Every nation that issues bonds has a **credit rating**, from AAA down to D. It comes from how well the nation's treasury covers the bonds it owes, and drops a notch for a shrinking economy or a weak government. A lower rating means riskier bonds, so they trade cheaper and pay a higher yield.

If a treasury can't pay a week's coupon or the final repayment, the nation **defaults**. Holders get their share of what the treasury holds, the bond stops paying, the nation is rated D, and it can't issue new bonds for 30 days. A default always makes the front page.

## For nation owners: the Treasury

If you own a nation, **Treasury** appears in the sidebar. From [the Treasury](/exchange/treasury) you can issue bonds:

- **Units**: 10 to 10,000, at ₷100 each.
- **Coupon**: 0.5% to 25% a year, paid weekly.
- **Term**: 1 to 12 weeks.

Money from units sold goes into your nation's treasury. The coupons and the final repayment come out of it, so issue only what your treasury can carry. A nation's outstanding bonds are capped at four times the treasury's cap.

## Companies and tenders

Charter and run a company from [My companies](/exchange/companies): capital, credit, decisions and dividends. Government tenders and company contracts are on [Tenders](/exchange/tenders), where companies bid and issuers award. Both need you to be signed in.
