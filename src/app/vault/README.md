# IxVault — Collectathon Hub: Wallet, Achievements, Cards & Store

**Last updated:** 2026-11-14

IxVault is the collectathon hub in IxStats: achievements, trading cards and the IxCredits store under one app, in the sidebar for every signed-in player. Players earn **IxCredits (IxC)** from platform activity (the daily claim, achievements, Lorewards, the nightly Vault dividend, card sales; never from a nation's performance or diplomacy), buy and open card packs, list, auction and trade cards, break them down, fuse and craft them, build showcases, and import their NationStates card decks. The `/vault` area is a page container (`AuthenticationGuard` → container) wrapping per-route section components; navigation uses normal Next.js routes, except inside the cards hub, whose four tabs switch in place (`useCardHubTab`, pushState).

There is no in-page Vault rail or duplicate sub-navigation: the global source list (`src/lib/navigation/app-sections.ts`) lists every destination (`Wallet`, `Daily reward`, `Store`, `Achievements`, `Lorewards`, `Leaderboards`, and the `Cards` group: `My cards`, `Sets`, `Market`, `Explore`, `Import deck`). `/leaderboards` renders a unified `<Card content="data" padding="none">` ranking table (`CountryLeaderboards` + `LeaderboardTab` collectors mode). The Wallet page (`/vault`) opens with a standard full-width `PageHeader` (`title="Wallet"`, `leading={<VaultMarkTile />}`, `actions={<StreakPill />}`) above the balance + net worth `LensCard` (`VaultBalanceCard` with embedded `DailyRewardTile` when ready), the velocity-projected milestones carousel (`VaultUnlocks`), the showcase pass shelf (`VaultShowcase`), and the full-width ledger statement (`VaultStatement`). The sidebar footer carries a compact copy of the balance, today's earnings and the daily reward control (`SidebarVaultCard`, with a wallet button on the collapsed rail); both show for every signed-in player.

## Key Features

- **Card packs** — The Store's Packs tab (`cardPacks.getAvailablePacks`, `purchasePack`; each pack shows its odds); My packs opens one in the short reveal (`PackReveal`, `cardPacks.openPack`). Pack types and odds are documented in `docs/systems/cards.md`.
- **Store and Market** — The Store (`VaultStoreTab`) sells packs, cosmetics and upgrades (`vault.listStoreItems` / `getPurchasedItems`). The cards hub's Market (`MarketPane`, `src/components/vault/cards/market/`) has Browse (live listings and auctions in one list, `cardMarket.browse`; ending soon is a sort), Trades (`trading.getActiveTrades`, with the history of trades and auctions; each trade opens the two-column trade builder, `TradeBuilderSheet`), Wishlist (`cardMarket.getWishlist`) and Selling (`getMyListings`, `getMyActiveAuctions`, `getMyActiveBids`, `createAuction`). Old Store `?tab=auctions|trading` links go to the Market through `src/proxy.ts`.
- **Showcases** — The hub's Sets tab (`SetsPane`): free-form showcases of copies (`cards.listMyShowcases`, `getShowcase`, `createShowcase`, `updateShowcase`, `deleteShowcase`, `addToShowcase`, `removeFromShowcase`, `pinShowcase`); the pinned one is the player's display (`getMyDisplay`).
- **The card workshop** — Break down copies for dust per realm, fuse duplicates into Foil and Holo, and craft missing cards for dust plus IxC (`crafting.getNumbers`, `getDust`, `breakDown`, `fuse`, `craft`), from the card sheet and Mine's select mode. The old recipe crafting was removed in Phase 1, and its old page redirects to Mine; see `docs/systems/cards.md`.
- **NationStates import** — Verify ownership and import an NS deck (`nsImport.requestVerification`, `checkVerification`, `importDeck`); see `docs/systems/ns-integration.md`.
- **Achievements and Lorewards** — `/vault/achievements` (see `docs/systems/achievements.md`); the Lorewards tab wraps the shared `LorewardsView` (`VaultLorewardsPanel`).
- **The Exchange (₷)** — its own app at `/exchange`, apart from the Vault; there is no IxC ⇄ ₷ conversion. See `docs/systems/exchange.md`.
- **IxCredits** — Earn (the daily claim, achievement unlocks, Lorewards payouts, NS deck import, card sales and the nightly Vault dividend; no country action pays) and spend (packs, the market, crafting, the store). Caps, formulas, and transaction types in `docs/systems/ixcredits.md`.

## Connections

- **Platform activity** — the nightly Vault dividend (`vault-dividend` cron) pays `EARN_PASSIVE` from collection interest, wiki edits (through a verified IxWiki link), ThinkPages and forum posts, and card activity; **Achievements / Lorewards / onboarding / NS import** pay uncapped `EARN_BONUS` via `grantBonus()`; the daily claim pays `EARN_ACTIVE`. Nothing a nation does earns IxCredits; the one country tie is the 2% nation-card royalty on a sale or a trade's IxC, paid out of the market fee. Every card's value comes from one formula (rarity floor, type, finish, the market median and the copy's level); the `card-values` cron writes the medians and each card's reference value.

## Architecture & Security Hardening (Plans 121–123)

- **Atomic Credit Ledger**: `spendCreditsTx` (`src/lib/vault/vault-ledger.ts`) executes atomic conditional updates (`credits: { gte: amount }`) inside DB transactions, preventing race conditions or negative balances under high concurrency.
- **UTC Calendar Day Streak Math**: Daily login streak calculations (`updateLoginStreak`) normalize dates onto UTC calendar day serial numbers (`Date.UTC(y, m, d) / 86,400,000`), ensuring exact midnight boundary rollover behavior.
- **Type-Safe Domain Modeling**: the card face's data is `src/lib/cards/face/types.ts` (`CardFaceData`); `CardInstance` (the marketplace types) and `CardAuthorInfo` stay in `src/types/cards-display.ts` (some `(db as any)` casts remain in routers).
- **Perk Performance Cache**: Store item perks lookup (`getPurchasedItemsEffects`) utilizes an in-memory `userPerksCache` (5-minute TTL) with bounded transaction queries (`take: 100`).
