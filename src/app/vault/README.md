# IxVault — Collectathon Hub: Wallet, Achievements, Cards & Store

**Last updated:** 2026-10-14

IxVault is the collectathon hub in IxStats: achievements, trading cards and the IxCredits store under one app, in the sidebar for every signed-in player. Players earn **IxCredits (IxC)** from platform activity (the daily claim, achievements, Lorewards, the nightly Vault dividend, card sales; never from a nation's performance or diplomacy), buy and open card packs, trade cards, run auctions, build showcases, and import their NationStates card decks. The `/vault` area is a page container (`AuthenticationGuard` → container) wrapping per-route section components; navigation uses normal Next.js routes, except inside the cards hub, whose four tabs switch in place (`useCardHubTab`, pushState).

There is no Vault rail: the global source list (`src/lib/navigation/app-sections.ts`) lists the sections. `/leaderboards` (the nation boards) renders in its own page container. The Wallet page (`/vault`) is a bank statement with some life in it: the page is titled "Vault" with the Vault mark (`VaultMarkTile`: `VaultLogomark` in a large tinted `IconWell`, decorative; the sidebar row stays "Wallet"): on desktop as the `<h1>` over the Your Vault list in the right column (`VaultTitle`, marked `shellPageTitleProps`), on phones as a phone-only `ShellPageHeader` with the mark as its `leading`, so one "Vault" shows at every width and no toolbar actions remain; the balance card (`VaultBalanceCard`: a counting IxCredits figure, collector level, the Claim daily reward button while claimable (the one primary), "+N today", streak, and one net worth well splitting credits from cards), the showcase (`VaultShowcase`: your top three cards by market value from `cards.getMyTopCards` on their real art, plus one pack card while packs wait), the statement (`VaultStatement`, a source icon on each row), the Your Vault list (`VaultHoldings`: Achievements, Lorewards, Cards and Store, each a row linking to its home), the achievements deck (`VaultUnlocks`: a swipeable deck of the three newest unlocks from `achievements.getMySummary.recent`, then the locked achievement the player is nearest to, with its progress, from `achievements.getNextUnlock`; hidden until there is either). On a phone the order is title, balance, achievements deck, Your Vault, showcase, statement. It reads no country data. The sidebar footer carries a compact copy of the balance, today's earnings and the daily reward control (`SidebarVaultCard`, with a wallet button on the collapsed rail); both show for every signed-in player.

## Key Features

- **Card packs** — Browse/purchase packs (`cardPacks.getAvailablePacks`, `purchasePack`), open via the cards pipeline. Pack types and odds are documented in `docs/systems/cards.md`.
- **Store and Market** — The Store (`VaultStoreTab`) sells packs, cosmetics and upgrades (`vault.listStoreItems` / `getPurchasedItems`). The cards hub's Market (`MarketPane`, `src/components/vault/cards/market/`) has Browse (`cardMarket.getActiveAuctions`; ending soon is a sort), Trades (`trading.getActiveTrades`, with the history of trades and auctions), Wishlist (a placeholder) and Selling (`getMyActiveAuctions`, `getMyActiveBids`, `createAuction`). Old Store `?tab=auctions|trading` links go to the Market through `src/proxy.ts`.
- **Showcases** — The hub's Sets tab (`SetsPane`): free-form showcases of copies (`cards.listMyShowcases`, `getShowcase`, `createShowcase`, `updateShowcase`, `deleteShowcase`, `addToShowcase`, `removeFromShowcase`, `pinShowcase`); the pinned one is the player's display (`getMyDisplay`).
- **Crafting** — Retired for now (2026-10-05): the `crafting` router and workbench are gone; see `docs/systems/cards.md`.
- **NationStates import** — Verify ownership and import an NS deck (`nsImport.requestVerification`, `checkVerification`, `importDeck`); see `docs/systems/ns-integration.md`.
- **Achievements and Lorewards** — `/vault/achievements` (see `docs/systems/achievements.md`); the Lorewards tab wraps the shared `LorewardsView` (`VaultLorewardsPanel`).
- **The Exchange (₷)** — lives in Labs at `/labs/exchange`, apart from the Vault; there is no IxC ⇄ ₷ conversion. See `docs/systems/exchange.md`.
- **IxCredits** — Earn (the daily claim, achievement unlocks, Lorewards payouts, NS deck import, card sales and the nightly Vault dividend; no country action pays) and spend (packs, market, store). Caps, formulas, and transaction types in `docs/systems/ixcredits.md`.

## Connections

- **Platform activity** — the nightly Vault dividend (`vault-dividend` cron) pays `EARN_PASSIVE` from collection interest, wiki edits (through a verified IxWiki link), ThinkPages and forum posts, and card activity; **Achievements / Lorewards / onboarding / NS import** pay uncapped `EARN_BONUS` via `grantBonus()`; the daily claim pays `EARN_ACTIVE`. Nothing a nation does earns IxCredits; the one country tie is the 2% nation-card royalty on an auction sale. NATION card values come from rarity and type (`card-values` cron).

## Architecture & Security Hardening (Plans 121–123)

- **Atomic Credit Ledger**: `spendCredits` executes atomic conditional updates (`credits: { gte: amount }`) inside DB transactions, preventing race conditions or negative balances under high concurrency.
- **UTC Calendar Day Streak Math**: Daily login streak calculations (`updateLoginStreak`) normalize dates onto UTC calendar day serial numbers (`Date.UTC(y, m, d) / 86,400,000`), ensuring exact midnight boundary rollover behavior.
- **Type-Safe Domain Modeling**: Branded domain primitives (`UserId`, `CardId`, `AuctionId`, `OwnershipId`) and structured schema interfaces (`ArtworkVariants`, `CardStatsData`, `CardEnhancementsData`) live in `src/types/cards-display.ts` (some `(db as any)` casts remain in routers).
- **Perk Performance Cache**: Store item perks lookup (`getPurchasedItemsEffects`) utilizes an in-memory `userPerksCache` (5-minute TTL) with bounded transaction queries (`take: 100`).
