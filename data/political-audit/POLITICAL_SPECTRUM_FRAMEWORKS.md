# IxStates — Political Spectrum Frameworks & Comparative Models

This document formalizes the comparative political theory models synthesized from the comprehensive audit of 4,725 articles across the MariaDB and PostgreSQL databases, specifically analyzing `Category:Politics` (48 subcategories, 688 articles) and `Category:Countries` (304 subcategories, 3,522 articles).

---

## Executive Summary: The Three Evaluated Frameworks

To replace legacy Left/Right real-world political labels, three distinct models were designed based strictly on empirical lore:

1. **Option 1: The Civic-Economic Compass** (`Order ⟷ Parity` × `Solidarity ⟷ Enterprise`)
2. **Option 2: The Authority & Purpose Dialectic** (`Fueros/Autonomy ⟷ Central Command` × `Sacral State ⟷ Material State`)
3. **Option 3: The Triad of Power (Selected Canonical Framework)** (`The Crown` / `The Altar` / `The Guild`)

## Option 1: The Civic-Economic Compass
### *The Structural Matrix Model (2D Coordinate Grid)*

```
                                [ SOLIDARITY ]
                       (Moral Duty / Shared Welfare)
                                     ▲
                                     │
           QUADRANT I                │               QUADRANT II
     CORPORATE PATERNALISM           │           CIVIC COLLECTIVISM
   (Guilds, Crown Welfare,           │       (Worker Councils, Egalitarian
    Catholic Distributism)           │        Societies, Tripartite Pacts)
                                     │
[ ORDER ] ───────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────── [ PARITY ]
(Estates, Guilds, Fueros,            │                 (Universal Law, Classless,
 Vestiary Ranks, Nobility)           │                  Popular Citizenship)
                                     │
           QUADRANT III              │               QUADRANT IV
      MERCANTILE ARISTOCRACY         │            CIVIC ENTERPRISE
   (Chartered Trade Cartels,         │        (Open Markets, Meritocracy,
    Thalattocratic Dominance)        │         Abolition of Guild Monopolies)
                                     │
                                     ▼
                               [ ENTERPRISE ]
                      (Commercial Wealth / Capital Growth)
```

### The Two Axes Defined
1. **Horizontal: `Order` ⟷ `Parity`**
   - **Order (High-Rank Society)**: Society is an organized hierarchy. Rights belong to your station, guild, nobility, military rank, or municipal charter (*fueros*).
   - **Parity (Equal-Citizen Society)**: Society is flat. All individuals possess equal legal standing; ancient caste privileges and guild monopolies are abolished.
2. **Vertical: `Solidarity` ⟷ `Enterprise`**
   - **Solidarity (Communal Welfare)**: The economy exists to guarantee social harmony, family stability, and moral health. Private profit is subordinate to the public good.
   - **Enterprise (Commercial Ambition)**: The economy exists to generate competitive wealth, maritime commerce, trade hegemony, and capital accumulation.

### Country Placements & Rationales
- **Urcea** (Quadrant I: Corporate Paternalism): Governed by the *Solidarity Party* and *Wittonian Socialism*. Mandatory guilds, family smallholders, and Catholic social protection.
- **Castadilla** (Quadrant I ⟷ II Border): Mediates between *Foralism* (peerages and fueros) and *Velvetine Socialism* (tripartite state-labor-enterprise pacts).
- **Carna** (Quadrant II: Civic Collectivism): *Bairdism*. Classless, non-partisan federation of bottom-up workers' councils (*Forgaitherin*).
- **Nasastan** (Quadrant II: Civic Collectivism): *Purokal* economic chamber operating under *Kaidoist* "final egalitarianism" with state-standardized wages.
- **Burgundie** (Quadrant III: Mercantile Aristocracy): *Consociational Thalattocracy* where admirals and prince-merchants coordinate under *Total Economic Engagement (TEE)*.
- **Caphiria** (Quadrant III: Mercantile Aristocracy): Enforces statutory *Vestiary Laws*. Governed by the Imperator, senatorial patricians, commercial *Sodalitates*, and the tetracameral *Corcillum*.
- **Kiravia** (Quadrant III ⟷ IV Border): Maritime federacy blending autonomous regional stanoras with dynamic merchant trading cartels.
- **Cartadania** (Quadrant IV: Civic Enterprise): Presidential syndicalist republic ("Sic Semper Tyrannis") with direct bicameral elections and rejection of hereditary titles.

## Option 2: The Authority & Purpose Dialectic
### *The Governance Source vs. Civilizational Telos Model*

```
                            [ THE SACRAL STATE ]
                       (Divine Mandate / Moral Order)
                                     ▲
                                     │
           POLE A                    │                    POLE B
    CONFESSIONAL CHARTER             │               SACRAL IMPERIUM
   (Urcea, Castadilla, Cape)         │            (Varshan, Caphiria)
                                     │
[ FUEROS / AUTONOMY ] ───────────────┼─────────────── [ CENTRAL COMMAND ]
(Subsidiarity, Local Diets,          │          (Unitary Imperator, Supreme
 Cantons, Ethnofederations)          │           Directorate, Basic Law)
                                     │
           POLE C                    │                    POLE D
   COMMERCIAL THALATTOCRACY          │           BUREAUCRATIC LEGALISM
     (Burgundie, Kiravia)            │              (Daxia, Puertego)
                                     │
                                     ▼
                           [ THE MATERIAL STATE ]
                    (Economic Power / Geopolitical Might)
```

### The Two Axes Defined
1. **Horizontal: `Fueros (Local Autonomy)` ⟷ `Central Command (Imperial State)`**
   - **Fueros / Autonomy**: Power is local and contractual. Municipalities, cantons, and member nations hold ancient charters and veto rights.
   - **Central Command**: Power is unitary and top-down. The central sovereign, senate, or directorate dictates policy across all provinces.
2. **Vertical: `The Sacral State` ⟷ `The Material State`**
   - **Sacral State (Moral Purpose)**: The state exists to serve a transcendent truth (Catholic salvation, Orthodox Arzalism, Zmoric harmony, Shaftonist virtue).
   - **Material State (Practical Power)**: The state exists to maximize trade volume, military prowess, industrial output, and worldly dominance.

### Country Placements & Rationales
- **Pole A (The Confessional Charter)**: *Urcea, Castadilla, Nasastan*. Subsidiarity, municipal fueros, and ethnofederal protection of ancestral communities.
- **Pole B (The Sacral Imperium)**: *Varshan, Caphiria*. Theocratic statehood under Orthodox Arzalism (*The Dazga*) or Caesaropapist Roman-derived Imperium.
- **Pole C (The Commercial Thalattocracy)**: *Burgundie, Kiravia*. Decentralized maritime provinces, port charters, and merchant assemblies prioritizing sea control and wealth.
- **Pole D (The Bureaucratic Legalist State)**: *Daxia, Puertego, Carna*. Unitary administrative apparatuses, People's Assemblies, and planned state direction.

## Option 3: The Triad of Power
### *The Selected Canonical Framework (Ternary Barycentric Model)*

```
                              [ THE CROWN ]
                        (Sovereign / State Command)
                                    / \
                                   /   \
                                  /     \
                                 /       \
                                /         \
                               /     ▲     \
                              /      │      \
                             /   (Nations)   \
                            /                 \
                           /                   \
                          /                     \
       [ THE ALTAR ] ───────────────────────────── [ THE GUILD ]
     (Sacred / Moral Law)                       (Workplace, Commons, Trade)
```

### The Three Pillars Defined
1. **The Crown (The Sovereign Principle)**: The central state, monarch, imperator, military assembly, and national directorate. *(Values: Order, Defense, Unity, Coercive Statecraft)*
2. **The Altar (The Moral Principle)**: The Church, Arzalist priesthood, Zmoric philosophers, and Shaftonist moralists. *(Values: Salvation, Justice, Interpersonal Trust, Moral Rectitude)*
3. **The Guild (The Productive Principle)**: The labor councils, merchant syndicates, provincial fueros, agricultural cooperatives, and trading leagues. *(Values: Livelihood, Local Rights, Commerce, Productive Self-Determination)*

### Mathematical Representation
Every political party, nation, or philosophy is represented as a barycentric coordinate triplet:
$$\text{Position} = (C, A, G) \quad \text{where} \quad C + A + G = 100\%$$
- $C$ = Crown Weight (State Authority, Executive Power, Imperium)
- $A$ = Altar Weight (Moral Law, Confessional Authority, Transcendental Mandate)
- $G$ = Guild Weight (Productive Autonomy, Commons, Syndicates, Municipal Charters)

### Core Archetypes on the Triangle
- **Pure Apex: The Crown (100% C)**: Autocratic Statism / Directorate (*Daxia, pure imperial statecraft*).
- **Pure Apex: The Altar (100% A)**: Hierocratic Theocracy (*Worshipful Realm of Varshan*).
- **Pure Apex: The Guild (100% G)**: Syndicalism / Council Communism (*Carna's Bairdism, Cartadanian Radical Syndicalism*).
- **Crown-Altar Edge (High C, High A, Low G)**: Sacral Monarchy / Imperial Faith (*Varshan, Caphiria*).
- **Crown-Guild Edge (High C, High G, Low A)**: Thalattocratic Statecraft / National Dirigisme (*Burgundie, Puertego*).
- **Altar-Guild Edge (High A, High G, Low C)**: Confessional Subsidiarity / Ethnocultural Autonomy (*Castadilla's Foralism, Nasastan's Kallistocracy*).
- **Center Tripartite Equilibrium (~33% C, 33% A, 33% G)**: Corporative Constitutionalism (*The Apostolic Kingdom of Urcea*).
