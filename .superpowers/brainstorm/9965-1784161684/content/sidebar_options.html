Sidebar Feed/Log Options

Choose how the Situation Room sidebar should display the national feed and story logs

A

Rich Story Feed Card Timeline (Recommended)

Replaces the default thin list with cards styled using FacetCard materials. Displays colorful gradient glyphs mapping to sources (Government ◉, World ◇, Press ▦), relative times, headline text, and optional story details.

B

Tabbed Feed & Change Ledger

Splits the sidebar into two mini-tabs: "News Feed" (Option A's story cards) and "Change Log" (a clean, scrollable tabular list of raw government ledger actions and budget deltas).
